## Detailed Item-by-Item Review

### Question 1
- **Domain:** `privacy`
- **Question:** Under Georgia evidence law (O.C.G.A. § 24-5-501, formerly § 24-9-21), which family communication is statutorily protected by evidentiary privilege on grounds of public policy?
- **Correct Answer:** A
- **Citation:** OCGA 24-9-21
- **Explanation:** O.C.G.A. § 24-5-501 (formerly § 24-9-21) lists communications between husband and wife among privileged communications that cannot be compelled on grounds of public policy.

### Question 2
- **Domain:** `privacy`
- **Question:** Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.07 (Rule 510-4-.02), when may psychologists disclose confidential, personally identifiable client information in writings, lectures, or other public media?
- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Citation:** APA 4.07
- **Explanation:** APA Standard 4.07 provides that psychologists do not disclose confidential, personally identifiable client information in writings, lectures, or public media unless they take reasonable steps to disguise the person or organization, obtain written consent, or have legal authorization.

### Question 3
- **Domain:** `assessment`
- **Question:** What must assessment instruments have for the population tested?
- **Correct Answer:** A
- **Citation:** APA 9.02 / Rule 510-4-.02
- **Explanation:** APA Ethics Code Standard 9.02 (Rule 510-4-.02) requires assessment instruments to have established validity and reliability for the population tested; when validity/reliability is not established, psychologists describe strengths and limitations of test results.

### Question 4
- **Domain:** `licensure`
- **Question:** What is the misdemeanor penalty for unlicensed practice of psychology?
- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Citation:** OCGA 43-39-19
- **Explanation:** O.C.G.A. § 43-39-19 provides that any person practicing psychology without a valid license is guilty of a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of $100 to $1,000, imprisonment up to 12 months, or both.

### Question 5
- **Domain:** `licensure`
- **Question:** How long is a Georgia temporary psychology license valid?
- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Citation:** OCGA 43-39-14; Rule 510-9-.01
- **Explanation:** O.C.G.A. § 43-39-14 and Rule 510-9-.01 establish that a temporary license is valid for 12 months from issuance and is non-renewable.

### Question 6
- **Domain:** `competence`
- **Question:** How often must IPC and TAP be renewed under PSYPACT?
- **Correct Answer:** D
- **Citation:** Rule 510-9-.03
- **Explanation:** Rule 510-9-.03 and PSYPACT regulations require ASPPB credentials (IPC, E.Passport) and PSYPACT authorities (TAP, APIT) to be renewed annually.

### Question 7
- **Domain:** `competence`
- **Question:** Under Rule 510-5-.07, must telepsychology services meet the same standard of care as in-person psychological services?
- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Citation:** Rule 510-5-.07
- **Explanation:** Board Rule 510-5-.07(2) and (5)(b)1 mandate that the provision of telepsychology must meet the same legal, ethical, and professional standards of care that are required when providing in-person psychological services.

### Question 8
- **Domain:** `competence`
- **Question:** How long is a physician's involuntary-treatment certificate valid?

### Question 9

- **Question:** What standard governs disclosures of confidential information without consent?
- **Correct Answer:** D
- **Citation:** APA 4.05 / Rule 510-4-.02
- **Explanation:** APA Ethics Code Standard 4.05 (Rule 510-4-.02) permits disclosures without consent only as mandated by law or permitted by law for a valid purpose, disclosing only the minimum necessary information.

### Question 10
