### Question 6
- **Domain:** `recordkeeping`
- **Question:** When is bartering for psychological services ethically permissible under Board rules?
- **Correct Answer:** A
- **Citation:** Rule 510-4-.02 Standard 6.05; Rule 510-5-.01

### Question 8
