# 2018 Georgia Code Title 43 - Professions and Businesses Chapter 39 - Psychologists § 43-39-4. Oath of office; certificate of appointment

- Immediately and before entering upon the duties of their office, the members of the board shall take the constitutional oath of office and shall file the same in the office of the Governor who, upon receiving said oath of office, shall issue to each member a certificate of appointment.
