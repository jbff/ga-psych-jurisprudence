# 2022 Georgia Code Title 16 - Crimes and Offenses Chapter 13 - Controlled Substances Article 5 - Sanctions Against Licensed Persons for Offenses Involving Controlled Substances or Marijuana § 16-13-113. Article as Supplement to Power of Licensing Authority

 The provisions of this article shall be supplemental to and shall not operate to prohibit any licensing authority from acting pursuant to those provisions of law which may now or hereafter authorize other sanctions and actions for that particular licensing authority. 

 History. Code 1981, § 16-13-113 , enacted by Ga. L. 1990, p. 2009, § 1.
