# 2022 Georgia Code Title 31 - Health Chapter 7 - Regulation and Construction of Hospitals and Other Health Care Facilities Article 8 - Health Service Provider Psychologists § 31-7-163. Status of Present Psychologist Staff Members

 Nothing in this article shall prohibit a psychologist currently a member of a hospital staff or an employee of a hospital from continuing to work in that capacity. 

 History. Code 1981, § 31-7-163 , enacted by Ga. L. 1983, p. 1426, § 2.
