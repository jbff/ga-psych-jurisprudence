# 2022 Georgia Code Title 43 - Professions and Businesses Chapter 1 - General Provisions § 43-1-22. Inactive Status Licenses

 The division director may provide for inactive status licenses for the various professional licensing boards. 

 History. Code 1981, § 43-1-22 , enacted by Ga. L. 1984, p. 552, § 1; Ga. L. 2000, p. 1706, § 8.
