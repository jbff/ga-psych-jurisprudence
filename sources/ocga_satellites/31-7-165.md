# 2022 Georgia Code Title 31 - Health Chapter 7 - Regulation and Construction of Hospitals and Other Health Care Facilities Article 8 - Health Service Provider Psychologists § 31-7-165. Report of Denial of Staff Privileges

 When any health service provider psychologist is denied staff privileges or is removed from the medical or professional staff, such action shall be reported by the facility to the State Board of Examiners of Psychologists. 

 History. Code 1981, § 31-7-165 , enacted by Ga. L. 1983, p. 1426, § 2.
