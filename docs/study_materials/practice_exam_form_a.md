## Section 1: Examination Questions

### Q1. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.02 (Rule 510-4-.02), when must psychologists discuss the relevant limits of confidentiality with clients?

- **A)** Only if the client formally requests written information concerning privacy policies
- **B)** Within 60 calendar days following the submission of the initial diagnostic insurance claim
- **C)** At the outset of the relationship and thereafter as new circumstances may warrant, unless not feasible or contraindicated
- **D)** Exclusively at the final termination session after all psychological services have concluded

### Q2. How many supportive recommendation letters are required for health service provider status?

- **A)** Two from health service providers in psychology
- **B)** Four letters from members of the Georgia State Board of Examiners
- **C)** One letter from an APA-accredited doctoral program director
- **D)** Three letters from licensed psychiatrists with active hospital admitting privileges

### Q3. Under O.C.G.A. § 31-7-164, may a medical facility limit, restrict, or revoke a psychologist's staff privileges for nondiscriminatory facility rule violations?

- **A)** Yes; privileges may be restricted or revoked for violating hospital rules applied in good faith and without discrimination
- **B)** No; privileges may only be revoked upon unanimous recommendation of an external state medical arbitration panel
- **C)** No; hospital privileges granted to psychologists are irrevocable property rights protected by constitutional due process
- **D)** Yes; but only if the psychologist has first been formally sanctioned or suspended by the Georgia State Board

### Q4. Under Board Rule 510-5-.06, what must a psychologist arrange when planning for a foreseeable temporary absence from practice?

- **A)** Surrender all active clinical case records to the Georgia Board of Examiners of Psychologists
- **B)** Instruct clients to refrain from seeking any mental health care until the psychologist returns
- **C)** Automatically discharge all active clients and require them to re-apply upon the psychologist's return
- **D)** Arrange for another qualified professional to be available to deal with emergency client needs

### Q5. Under Board Rule 510-5-.03, how are licensed psychologists required to maintain their professional competency?

- **A)** By submitting annual taped clinical psychotherapy sessions to the Board for peer review
- **B)** Through continuing education, professional consultation, and activities consistent with current standards
- **C)** By retaking and passing the national EPPP examination every five years
- **D)** Exclusively by subscribing to and reading peer-reviewed empirical research journals

### Q6. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 6.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), may psychologists withhold records under their control needed for emergency treatment solely because payment has not been received?

- **A)** Yes; psychologists retain an absolute statutory lien on all patient records until paid
- **B)** Yes; provided the client has been given 30 days written notice of collection referral
- **C)** No; psychologists may never withhold records under any circumstances, even for routine non-emergency requests
- **D)** No; psychologists may not withhold records requested and needed for emergency treatment solely for nonpayment

### Q7. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 8.02(b) (Rule 510-4-.02), what additional information must psychologists clarify to participants in intervention research involving experimental treatments?

- **A)** An agreement that participants in the control group will be permanently denied effective treatment
- **B)** Experimental nature, control group services, assignment method, treatment alternatives, and costs/compensation
- **C)** Personal psychiatric histories of the clinical research investigators and data monitoring board members
- **D)** Guaranteed therapeutic cure rates, institutional copyright licenses, and commercial marketing strategies

### Q8. What must psychologists do before consulting about a client?

- **A)** Not disclose identifying information unless consent obtained or unavoidable, and limit to necessary extent
- **B)** Obtain a notarized release signed by the client and both of the client's parents
- **C)** Disclose the client's full legal name, social security number, and home address
- **D)** Conduct the consultation exclusively in public professional peer review meetings

### Q9. When should psychologists terminate therapy?

- **A)** When no longer needed, not likely to benefit, or being harmed; also if threatened
- **B)** Whenever the client expresses disagreement with the psychologist's personal beliefs
- **C)** Exclusively when the client exhausts private health insurance benefits
- **D)** Only when the psychologist retires or relocates to another state

### Q10. Under O.C.G.A. § 30-5-4, are financial institution employees mandatory reporters of suspected financial exploitation of elder persons?

- **A)** Yes; financial institution employees must report, but the obligation does not apply while acting as a fiduciary for assets held in that capacity
- **B)** Yes; financial institution employees must report all suspected exploitation with zero exceptions for fiduciary asset management
- **C)** No; financial institutions are governed exclusively by federal banking regulations and exempt from state reporting
- **D)** No; financial institution employees are encouraged to report but face no mandatory reporting obligation under Georgia law

### Q11. Under Rule 510-5-.08, what must forensic psychologists do regarding multiple or potentially conflicting roles in legal proceedings?

- **A)** Avoid multiple and potentially conflicting roles; if unavoidable, clarify role expectations and confidentiality in advance
- **B)** Accept contingent fee bonuses based upon favorable trial judgments for the retaining party
- **C)** Freely combine treating psychotherapist and court-appointed custody evaluator roles to maximize insight
- **D)** Serve as both expert witness and legal co-counsel when authorized by the presiding trial judge

### Q12. Under Rule 510-5-.07, what emergency resources must telepsychology practitioners identify and learn how to access?

- **A)** Relevant and appropriate emergency resources in the client's local geographic area
- **B)** The emergency department closest to the psychologist's primary physical office location
- **C)** National crisis call centers without identifying any local emergency responders
- **D)** The immediate supervisor or designated colleague on call at the psychologist's clinic

### Q13. What percentage of internship supervision must be by licensed psychologists?

- **A)** At least 80%
- **B)** 100%
- **C)** At least 65%
- **D)** At least 50%

### Q14. Under Rule 510-6-.02, what finding standard is required for the Board to impose disciplinary sanctions upon a licensee or applicant?

- **A)** Concurrence of the Board President and the Division Director alone
- **B)** Unanimous vote of all appointed board members present and voting
- **C)** A finding by a majority of the Board that the licensee or applicant committed a statutory violation
- **D)** A two-thirds supermajority vote of the entire board confirmed by the Governor

### Q15. Under Rule 510-5-.08, what must a forensic psychologist do when an individual examination is not feasible despite reasonable efforts?

- **A)** Petition the presiding judge to issue a bench warrant compelling the individual's appearance
- **B)** Decline to testify or produce any written work product under any circumstances
- **C)** Clarify the impact of limited information on reliability/validity and appropriately limit conclusions
- **D)** Substitute diagnostic conclusions reached by treating clinicians without disclosing the absence of examination

### Q16. What must assessment instruments have for the population tested?

- **A)** Established validity and reliability; if not, describe limitations
- **B)** Public-domain status allowing unrestricted photocopying of test stimuli
- **C)** Universal cultural applicability without need for normative adaptations
- **D)** Formal endorsement and certification by the Georgia Department of Education

### Q17. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-4(c), what distinguishes aggravated child molestation from child molestation, and what penalty applies?

- **A)** Commission on school grounds; punishable by 10 to 20 years imprisonment
- **B)** Involving an act of sodomy or causing physical injury to a child under 16; punishable by 25 to 50 years or life imprisonment
- **C)** Commission by an educator or therapist; punishable by 15 to 30 years imprisonment
- **D)** Involving electronic photography; punishable by 5 to 15 years imprisonment

### Q18. Under Board Rule 510-9-.04, what continuing education is required to obtain a license for volunteer service?

- **A)** 12 hours in the two years immediately prior to application
- **B)** 40 hours in the two years immediately prior to application
- **C)** 20 hours in the two years immediately prior to application
- **D)** 24 hours in the two years immediately prior to application

### Q19. What is psychologists' responsibility regarding test security?

- **A)** Release test manuals and test protocols to attorneys upon receipt of a standard civil subpoena
- **B)** Make reasonable efforts to maintain integrity and security consistent with law
- **C)** Discard all test protocols immediately after scoring to prevent third-party access
- **D)** Test questions and protocols should be made available online for public transparency

### Q20. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 6.01 (Rule 510-4-.02), what are the primary purposes for which psychologists must create and maintain professional records?

- **A)** To facilitate later services, replicate research, meet institutional rules, ensure accurate billing, and comply with law
- **B)** To provide law enforcement agencies with automatic weekly summaries of all patient admissions and discharges
- **C)** To enable public marketing of anonymized client success stories across social media and institutional websites
- **D)** To guarantee third-party commercial insurers absolute and unrestricted access to all therapy session notes

### Q21. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-5.1(d), is client consent an admissible defense to criminal prosecution for improper sexual contact by a psychotherapist?

- **A)** Yes; client consent is an absolute defense provided both parties are competent adults over age twenty-one
- **B)** No; unless the psychotherapeutic relationship was formally terminated in writing at least six months prior
- **C)** No; consent of the victim shall not be a defense to criminal prosecution under this statute
- **D)** Yes; documented informed consent signed prior to the sexual contact serves as a complete affirmative defense

### Q22. How long may the postdoctoral SWE extend?

- **A)** No less than 11 months and no more than 24 months
- **B)** No less than 6 months and no more than 12 months
- **C)** No less than 12 months and no more than 36 months
- **D)** No less than 18 months and no more than 30 months

### Q23. What is the penalty for failure to notify the licensing authority of a drug conviction?

- **A)** A mandatory $500 civil fine with no license action
- **B)** Grounds for revocation
- **C)** Requirement to complete 10 additional ethics CE credits
- **D)** Automatic 30-day administrative suspension

### Q24. Under Rule 510-6-.02, what behaviors constitute unprofessional conduct under OCGA 43-1-19(a)(6)?

- **A)** Any act or practice violating the Board Code of Ethics or Code of Conduct, including acts by supervisees
- **B)** Failure to pay annual dues to the Georgia Psychological Association or local professional chapter
- **C)** Billing rates that deviate by more than ten percent from prevailing local community market averages
- **D)** Only acts resulting in a felony criminal indictment or civil malpractice money judgment

### Q25. What chapters constitute the Georgia psychology Code of Ethics and Supplemental Code of Conduct?

- **A)** Chapters 510-1 and 510-2
- **B)** Chapters 510-2 and 510-3
- **C)** Chapters 510-4 and 510-5
- **D)** Chapters 510-6 and 510-7

### Q26. What must psychologists base assessment opinions on?

- **A)** Statistical averages of the demographic group without individualized testing
- **B)** Testimonials and collateral reports from interested third-party family members
- **C)** Intuitive clinical impressions gathered during an informal initial meeting
- **D)** Information and techniques sufficient to substantiate findings

### Q27. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-3(b), what penalty is mandated upon a second or subsequent conviction for felony statutory rape in Georgia?

- **A)** Imprisonment for 5 to 10 years with standard administrative parole eligibility
- **B)** A non-negotiable fine of $50,000 and 3 years in a county correctional diversion center
- **C)** Indefinite civil commitment under the Sexually Dangerous Predator Act
- **D)** Mandatory sentence of life imprisonment, or a split sentence with life probation and electronic monitoring

### Q28. What must psychologists plan for when interrupting psychological services?

- **A)** Facilitating services in event of illness, death, unavailability, relocation, or financial limitations
- **B)** Immediately refunding all historical fees collected throughout the entire treatment course
- **C)** Automatically terminating all clients without notice upon reaching age sixty-five
- **D)** Transferring all active clinical files to the local county courthouse for storage

### Q29. What is 'sexual contact' under OCGA 16-6-5.1?

- **A)** Non-physical verbal comments or sexual innuendos delivered during a therapy session
- **B)** Contact involving intimate parts of either person for sexual gratification
- **C)** Standard physical touch involved in taking vital signs or neurological reflexes
- **D)** Any consensual social dating relationship established two years after termination

### Q30. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 5.02 (Rule 510-4-.02), who retains professional responsibility for public statements created or placed by others to promote a psychologist's practice?

- **A)** The media publisher or platform host that broadcast or printed the promotional material
- **B)** The marketing firm or public relations agency that authored and published the advertisement
- **C)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists upon approving the promotional copy
- **D)** The psychologist retains full professional responsibility for all such promotional statements

### Q31. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), what is required before recording the voices or images of clients receiving psychological services?

- **A)** Obtaining approval from the Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists ethics committee
- **B)** Providing verbal notice at least twenty-four hours after the recorded clinical session has concluded
- **C)** Ensuring the recording is stored on an unencrypted removable USB flash drive for seven years
- **D)** Obtaining permission from all clients or their legal representatives prior to recording

### Q32. Who may bring an injunction for unlicensed practice?

- **A)** The Georgia Department of Public Health
- **B)** The Board
- **C)** Any aggrieved consumer or client
- **D)** The Georgia Psychological Association

### Q33. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), what conduct is prohibited regarding unfair discrimination and harassment?

- **A)** Referring clients with complex medical comorbidities to board-certified medical specialists
- **B)** Selecting specialized psychological assessment batteries based on the client's primary language
- **C)** Charging different sliding-scale hourly professional fees based on verified household income levels
- **D)** Unfair discrimination and harassment or demeaning conduct based on protected personal characteristics

### Q34. Under Board Rule 510-5-.07, must telepsychology services adhere to the same professional standards and rules as in-person psychological practice?

- **A)** Yes; but only when services are delivered to adult clients who reside within Georgia
- **B)** Yes; telepsychology must adhere to the exact same legal, ethical, and clinical practice standards as in-person services
- **C)** No; telepsychology practice is subject to relaxed standards of documentation and clinical recordkeeping
- **D)** No; telepsychology is exempt from state confidentiality and informed consent rules

### Q35. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 8.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), when may psychologists record voices or images in research without obtaining prior informed consent?

- **A)** Whenever the research is conducted within private healthcare facilities or residential treatment centers
- **B)** When research consists of naturalistic observations in public places without personal identification/harm, or deception with debriefing consent
- **C)** Only when the research subjects are minor children whose school teachers have granted verbal authorization
- **D)** Whenever the recorded video footage is broadcast exclusively on password-protected commercial websites

### Q36. Under O.C.G.A. § 19-7-5(e), within what mandatory timeframe must a psychologist make a report of suspected child abuse?

- **A)** Within 7 calendar days, followed by a formal notarized report within 30 days
- **B)** Immediately, but in no case later than 24 hours from the time there is reasonable cause to believe suspected child abuse occurred
- **C)** Within 72 hours from the time there is reasonable cause to believe suspected child abuse occurred
- **D)** Within 48 hours from the time there is reasonable cause to believe suspected child abuse occurred

### Q37. Under Board Rule 510-5-.10, may a Georgia psychologist employ or supervise an individual who has voluntarily surrendered a psychology license under disciplinary proceedings?

- **A)** No; supervision or employment in any capacity is prohibited unless the Board waives or modifies this restriction for extraordinary circumstances
- **B)** No; the Board is statutorily prohibited from granting an extraordinary circumstances waiver under any conditions
- **C)** Yes; provided the supervisee practices exclusively in private outpatient clinics and signs a supervision agreement
- **D)** Yes; employment is permissible automatically after a mandatory two-year waiting period

### Q38. What standard governs disclosures of confidential information without consent?

- **A)** Only as mandated by law or permitted by law for valid purposes, limited to the minimum necessary
- **B)** Psychologists may disclose confidential information whenever requested by family members
- **C)** Disclosures without consent are strictly prohibited under every statutory provision
- **D)** Disclosures are permitted whenever advantageous to the psychologist's academic research

### Q39. Under Rule 510-5-.08, how must psychologists testify and present forensic reports?

- **A)** Truthfully, honestly, and candidly, while describing fairly the factual basis for testimony
- **B)** Exclusively by offering definitive legal conclusions on the ultimate legal issue before the court
- **C)** In strict conformity with the pre-trial witness coaching provided by the retaining attorney
- **D)** Zealously as an advocate for the party who retained and compensated the psychologist

### Q40. Under OCGA 16-13-110, what dispositions are included within the definition of 'convicted' for licensed-person drug sanctions?

- **A)** Entry of formal criminal indictment or accusation by a county prosecuting attorney
- **B)** Any arrest for a felony drug offense resulting in formal booking and cash bail
- **C)** Final conviction, plea of guilty or nolo contendere, or first offender treatment
- **D)** Final conviction following exhaustion of all state and federal appellate reviews

### Q41. Under O.C.G.A. § 30-5-4(b)(2), by what means may a mandated report of suspected disabled adult or elder abuse be submitted?

- **A)** Exclusively by formal verified petition filed in the probate court
- **B)** Exclusively by certified mail with return receipt requested
- **C)** By oral or written communication
- **D)** Exclusively by in-person personal appearance at the county adult protection office

### Q42. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-18, how does the Georgia General Assembly legally declare the unlicensed practice of psychology?

- **A)** A minor administrative infraction punishable solely by an informational warning letter
- **B)** A common law tort actionable only if an individual patient proves financial damages
- **C)** An exempt commercial business activity governed exclusively by the Federal Trade Commission
- **D)** A menace, nuisance, and dangerous to the public health, safety, and welfare

### Q43. After two years, who bears the burden of showing no exploitation with a former client sexual relationship?

- **A)** The American Psychological Association Ethics Committee
- **B)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners
- **C)** The former client who alleges exploitation
- **D)** The psychologist

### Q44. Under Board Rule 510-5-.07, must telepsychology services meet the same standard of care as in-person psychological services?

- **A)** No; telepsychology standards of care apply only when billing commercial health insurance plans
- **B)** Yes; but lower standards of care apply during the maintenance phase following initial evaluation
- **C)** No; remote practice is subject to relaxed standards of care due to technological constraints
- **D)** Yes; telepsychology must meet the same ethical, legal, and professional standards of care as in-person services

### Q45. Who should be principal author on a dissertation-based article?

- **A)** Authorship must always be listed strictly alphabetically regardless of contribution
- **B)** The department chairperson who secured grant funding for the laboratory
- **C)** The student, except under exceptional circumstances
- **D)** The dissertation faculty advisor who supervised the research design

### Q46. Under O.C.G.A. § 37-3-41(a), within what timeframe must a peace officer act to take a person into custody upon receiving an emergency certificate?

- **A)** Within 24 hours after receiving the certificate
- **B)** Within 96 hours after receiving the certificate
- **C)** Within 48 hours after receiving the certificate
- **D)** Within 72 hours after receiving the certificate

### Q47. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 10.06 (Rule 510-4-.02), may psychologists engage in sexual intimacies with close relatives, guardians, or significant others of current clients?

- **A)** Yes; provided the client gives prior written permission and therapy is temporarily paused
- **B)** Yes; because relatives and significant others are not direct clients of the psychologist
- **C)** No; unless the relative or significant other resides in a different household from the client
- **D)** No; psychologists are prohibited from sexual intimacies with known close relatives, guardians, or significant others of current clients

### Q48. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-1-21, may the division director disclose information regarding past or pending investigations to other licensing authorities?

- **A)** No; investigative information is strictly confidential and cannot be disclosed before a formal consent order is executed
- **B)** Yes; but only if the investigated licensee executes a notarized consent form authorizing the disclosure
- **C)** Yes; the division director is authorized to provide investigative and disciplinary information to any lawful licensing authority upon inquiry
- **D)** No; disclosure to out-of-state licensing authorities requires a formal federal grand jury subpoena

### Q49. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.04 (Rule 510-4-.02), what information must psychologists include in written and oral reports and consultations?

- **A)** All raw psychological test responses, protocols, and psychometric scores obtained during the evaluation
- **B)** A comprehensive developmental, educational, and medical history regardless of referral question
- **C)** All historical diagnostic impressions recorded across the client's lifetime by previous treatment providers
- **D)** Only information germane to the specific purpose for which the communication is made

### Q50. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 6.02 (Rule 510-4-.02), what precaution must psychologists take when entering confidential client records into databases accessible to non-consenting persons?

- **A)** Use coding or other de-identification techniques to avoid the inclusion of personal identifiers
- **B)** File a copy of all electronic database credentials with the Georgia Board of Examiners of Psychologists
- **C)** Require the database administrator to obtain an active Georgia psychology license before access is granted
- **D)** Obtain a court subpoena before uploading any diagnostic codes into electronic health record systems
