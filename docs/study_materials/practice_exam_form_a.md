## Section 1: Examination Questions

### Q1. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 6.01 (Rule 510-4-.02), what are the primary purposes for which psychologists must create and maintain professional records?

- **A)** To enable public marketing of anonymized client success stories across social media and institutional websites
- **B)** To facilitate later services, replicate research, meet institutional rules, ensure accurate billing, and comply with law
- **C)** To guarantee third-party commercial insurers absolute and unrestricted access to all therapy session notes
- **D)** To provide law enforcement agencies with automatic weekly summaries of all patient admissions and discharges

### Q2. Under Board Rule 510-5-.06, what must a psychologist arrange when planning for a foreseeable temporary absence from practice?

- **A)** Arrange for another qualified professional to be available to deal with emergency client needs
- **B)** Automatically discharge all active clients and require them to re-apply upon the psychologist's return
- **C)** Instruct clients to refrain from seeking any mental health care until the psychologist returns
- **D)** Surrender all active clinical case records to the Georgia Board of Examiners of Psychologists

### Q3. May psychologists withhold records solely because payment has not been received?

- **A)** Yes, except when the client is involved in active bankruptcy proceedings
- **B)** Yes, provided the outstanding fee balance has been formally referred to a collection agency
- **C)** No, not records needed for emergency treatment
- **D)** Yes; psychologists retain an absolute common-law artisan lien on all records until paid

### Q4. Under Rule 510-5-.07, must telepsychology services meet the same standard of care as in-person psychological services?

- **A)** Only when services are billed to Medicaid or state employee benefit health plans
- **B)** Only during the initial diagnostic assessment, after which lower standards of care apply
- **C)** No; remote practice is subject to relaxed standards of care due to technological constraints
- **D)** Yes; telepsychology must meet the same ethical, legal, and professional standards of care as in-person services

### Q5. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 8.02(b) (Rule 510-4-.02), what additional information must psychologists clarify to participants in intervention research involving experimental treatments?

- **A)** Guaranteed therapeutic cure rates, institutional copyright licenses, and commercial marketing strategies
- **B)** Personal psychiatric histories of the clinical research investigators and data monitoring board members
- **C)** Experimental nature, control group services, assignment method, treatment alternatives, and costs/compensation
- **D)** An agreement that participants in the control group will be permanently denied effective treatment

### Q6. Do the Georgia codes apply to applicants and students?

- **A)** No; students and applicants are governed solely by university honor codes
- **B)** Only to postdoctoral fellows who have completed doctoral degrees
- **C)** No; the codes apply strictly to fully licensed psychologists holding active licenses
- **D)** Yes; they apply to all licensees and applicants, including conduct during required education/training/employment

### Q7. What standard governs disclosures of confidential information without consent?

- **A)** Disclosures are permitted whenever advantageous to the psychologist's academic research
- **B)** Psychologists may disclose confidential information whenever requested by family members
- **C)** Disclosures without consent are strictly prohibited under every statutory provision
- **D)** Only as mandated by law or permitted by law for valid purposes, limited to the minimum necessary

### Q8. What chapters constitute the Georgia psychology Code of Ethics and Supplemental Code of Conduct?

- **A)** Chapters 510-6 and 510-7
- **B)** Chapters 510-2 and 510-3
- **C)** Chapters 510-1 and 510-2
- **D)** Chapters 510-4 and 510-5

### Q9. Under Rule 510-6-.02, what finding standard is required for the Board to impose disciplinary sanctions upon a licensee or applicant?

- **A)** A two-thirds supermajority vote of the entire board confirmed by the Governor
- **B)** Concurrence of the Board President and the Division Director alone
- **C)** Unanimous vote of all appointed board members present and voting
- **D)** A finding by a majority of the Board that the licensee or applicant committed a statutory violation

### Q10. When should psychologists terminate therapy?

- **A)** Only when the psychologist retires or relocates to another state
- **B)** When no longer needed, not likely to benefit, or being harmed; also if threatened
- **C)** Exclusively when the client exhausts private health insurance benefits
- **D)** Whenever the client expresses disagreement with the psychologist's personal beliefs

### Q11. Under OCGA 30-5-4, are financial institution employees mandatory reporters of suspected elder exploitation?

- **A)** No; financial institutions are governed exclusively by federal banking privacy regulations
- **B)** Yes, with no statutory exceptions or exemptions for fiduciary roles or trust management
- **C)** Only when suspected fraudulent transactions involve wire transfers exceeding $100,000
- **D)** Yes, but the reporting obligation does not apply while acting as a fiduciary for assets held in that capacity

### Q12. May the division director disclose information regarding pending investigations to other licensing authorities?

- **A)** Only if the investigated licensee executes a notarized consent form authorizing the disclosure
- **B)** Only upon issuance of a formal subpoena by a federal regulatory or law enforcement agency
- **C)** No; investigative information is strictly confidential and cannot be disclosed before a formal consent order is executed
- **D)** Yes; authorized to provide investigative and disciplinary information to any lawful licensing authority upon inquiry

### Q13. What percentage of internship supervision must be by licensed psychologists?

- **A)** 100%
- **B)** At least 50%
- **C)** At least 65%
- **D)** At least 80%

### Q14. Under Board Rule 510-9-.04, what continuing education is required to obtain a license for volunteer service?

- **A)** 40 hours in the two years immediately prior to application
- **B)** 24 hours in the two years immediately prior to application
- **C)** 12 hours in the two years immediately prior to application
- **D)** 20 hours in the two years immediately prior to application

### Q15. What must psychologists do before consulting about a client?

- **A)** Obtain a notarized release signed by the client and both of the client's parents
- **B)** Conduct the consultation exclusively in public professional peer review meetings
- **C)** Not disclose identifying information unless consent obtained or unavoidable, and limit to necessary extent
- **D)** Disclose the client's full legal name, social security number, and home address

### Q16. Under Board Rule 510-10-.03, under what legal conditions may psychology students or trainees administer and interpret psychological tests?

- **A)** Only when they have completed at least two years of doctoral coursework and hold written authorization from ASPPB
- **B)** Without supervision, provided the test scores are reviewed and co-signed by an agency administrative director
- **C)** Only when administering standardized objective tests that do not involve projective techniques or clinical interviews
- **D)** When they satisfy requisite statutory and rule requirements and practice under direct supervision of a licensed psychologist

### Q17. Under Rule 510-5-.08, what must forensic psychologists do regarding multiple or potentially conflicting roles in legal proceedings?

- **A)** Serve as both expert witness and legal co-counsel when authorized by the presiding trial judge
- **B)** Avoid multiple and potentially conflicting roles; if unavoidable, clarify role expectations and confidentiality in advance
- **C)** Accept contingent fee bonuses based upon favorable trial judgments for the retaining party
- **D)** Freely combine treating psychotherapist and court-appointed custody evaluator roles to maximize insight

### Q18. What action is required when a health facility denies or revokes health service provider staff privileges?

- **A)** Within 60 days to the county medical society and district attorney
- **B)** Privileges actions are internal hospital matters and need not be reported
- **C)** Reported by the facility to the State Board of Examiners of Psychologists
- **D)** Within 180 days to the American Psychological Association Ethics Committee

### Q19. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.02 (Rule 510-4-.02), when must psychologists discuss the relevant limits of confidentiality with clients?

- **A)** Only if the client formally requests written information concerning privacy policies
- **B)** Exclusively at the final termination session after all psychological services have concluded
- **C)** Within 60 calendar days following the submission of the initial diagnostic insurance claim
- **D)** At the outset of the relationship and thereafter as new circumstances may warrant, unless not feasible or contraindicated

### Q20. How many supportive recommendation letters are required for health service provider status?

- **A)** Four letters from members of the Georgia State Board of Examiners
- **B)** Two from health service providers in psychology
- **C)** Three letters from licensed psychiatrists with active hospital admitting privileges
- **D)** One letter from an APA-accredited doctoral program director

### Q21. Under Rule 510-5-.08, what must a forensic psychologist do when an individual examination is not feasible despite reasonable efforts?

- **A)** Clarify the impact of limited information on reliability/validity and appropriately limit conclusions
- **B)** Decline to testify or produce any written work product under any circumstances
- **C)** Substitute diagnostic conclusions reached by treating clinicians without disclosing the absence of examination
- **D)** Petition the presiding judge to issue a bench warrant compelling the individual's appearance

### Q22. Under OCGA 31-7-164, may a medical facility limit, restrict, or revoke a psychologist's staff privileges for nondiscriminatory rule violations?

- **A)** No; hospital privileges granted to psychologists are irrevocable property rights protected by due process
- **B)** Yes; privileges may be restricted or revoked for violating hospital rules applied in good faith and without discrimination
- **C)** Only following unanimous recommendation of the hospital's external psychology advisory panel
- **D)** Only if the psychologist is also formally disciplined or suspended by the Georgia State Board

### Q23. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-5.1(d), is client consent an admissible defense to criminal prosecution for improper sexual contact by a psychotherapist?

- **A)** Yes; documented informed consent signed prior to the sexual contact serves as a complete affirmative defense
- **B)** Yes; client consent is an absolute defense provided both parties are competent adults over age twenty-one
- **C)** No; unless the psychotherapeutic relationship was formally terminated in writing at least six months prior
- **D)** No; consent of the victim shall not be a defense to criminal prosecution under this statute

### Q24. How long may the postdoctoral SWE extend?

- **A)** No less than 6 months and no more than 12 months
- **B)** No less than 11 months and no more than 24 months
- **C)** No less than 18 months and no more than 30 months
- **D)** No less than 12 months and no more than 36 months

### Q25. What is the penalty for failure to notify the licensing authority of a drug conviction?

- **A)** Requirement to complete 10 additional ethics CE credits
- **B)** A mandatory $500 civil fine with no license action
- **C)** Grounds for revocation
- **D)** Automatic 30-day administrative suspension

### Q26. Under Rule 510-6-.02, what behaviors constitute unprofessional conduct under OCGA 43-1-19(a)(6)?

- **A)** Any act or practice violating the Board Code of Ethics or Code of Conduct, including acts by supervisees
- **B)** Failure to pay annual dues to the Georgia Psychological Association or local professional chapter
- **C)** Billing rates that deviate by more than ten percent from prevailing local community market averages
- **D)** Only acts resulting in a felony criminal indictment or civil malpractice money judgment

### Q27. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.02(c) (Rule 510-4-.02), what must psychologists inform clients about when offering services via electronic transmission?

- **A)** The foreseeable risks to privacy and the specific limits of confidentiality inherent in electronic transmission
- **B)** That the Georgia State Board monitors all electronic telepsychology sessions in real time
- **C)** That privilege does not apply to any communication transmitted electronically or by telephone
- **D)** A guarantee that electronic transmission over the Internet carries zero risk of interception

### Q28. Under Board Rule 510-9-.01, what is the maximum duration for which a temporary license remains in effect?

- **A)** A maximum of 6 months
- **B)** A maximum of 24 months
- **C)** A maximum of 3 months
- **D)** A maximum of 12 months

### Q29. May psychologists promote assessment use by unqualified persons?

- **A)** Yes, to reduce clinical assessment backlogs in public community clinics
- **B)** Yes, whenever unqualified persons purchase automated computer scoring software
- **C)** Yes, provided the unqualified person charges reduced administrative fees
- **D)** No, except for training with appropriate supervision

### Q30. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-18, how does the Georgia General Assembly legally declare the unlicensed practice of psychology?

- **A)** A minor administrative infraction punishable solely by an informational warning letter
- **B)** A menace, nuisance, and dangerous to the public health, safety, and welfare
- **C)** An exempt commercial business activity governed exclusively by the Federal Trade Commission
- **D)** A common law tort actionable only if an individual patient proves financial damages

### Q31. What must psychologists plan for when interrupting psychological services?

- **A)** Immediately refunding all historical fees collected throughout the entire treatment course
- **B)** Automatically terminating all clients without notice upon reaching age sixty-five
- **C)** Facilitating services in event of illness, death, unavailability, relocation, or financial limitations
- **D)** Transferring all active clinical files to the local county courthouse for storage

### Q32. Under Rule 510-5-.08, how must psychologists testify and present forensic reports?

- **A)** In strict conformity with the pre-trial witness coaching provided by the retaining attorney
- **B)** Exclusively by offering definitive legal conclusions on the ultimate legal issue before the court
- **C)** Zealously as an advocate for the party who retained and compensated the psychologist
- **D)** Truthfully, honestly, and candidly, while describing fairly the factual basis for testimony

### Q33. What is 'sexual contact' under OCGA 16-6-5.1?

- **A)** Contact involving intimate parts of either person for sexual gratification
- **B)** Non-physical verbal comments or sexual innuendos delivered during a therapy session
- **C)** Standard physical touch involved in taking vital signs or neurological reflexes
- **D)** Any consensual social dating relationship established two years after termination

### Q34. Who may bring an injunction for unlicensed practice?

- **A)** Any aggrieved consumer or client
- **B)** The Georgia Psychological Association
- **C)** The Georgia Department of Public Health
- **D)** The Board

### Q35. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), what conduct is prohibited regarding unfair discrimination and harassment?

- **A)** Referring clients with complex medical comorbidities to board-certified medical specialists
- **B)** Charging different sliding-scale hourly professional fees based on verified household income levels
- **C)** Selecting specialized psychological assessment batteries based on the client's primary language
- **D)** Unfair discrimination and harassment or demeaning conduct based on protected personal characteristics

### Q36. Under O.C.G.A. § 37-3-41(a), within what timeframe must a peace officer act to take a person into custody upon receiving an emergency certificate?

- **A)** Within 24 hours after receiving the certificate
- **B)** Within 96 hours after receiving the certificate
- **C)** Within 48 hours after receiving the certificate
- **D)** Within 72 hours after receiving the certificate

### Q37. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 8.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), when may psychologists record voices or images in research without obtaining prior informed consent?

- **A)** Only when the research subjects are minor children whose school teachers have granted verbal authorization
- **B)** When research consists of naturalistic observations in public places without personal identification/harm, or deception with debriefing consent
- **C)** Whenever the research is conducted within private healthcare facilities or residential treatment centers
- **D)** Whenever the recorded video footage is broadcast exclusively on password-protected commercial websites

### Q38. Under O.C.G.A. § 19-7-5(e), within what mandatory timeframe must a psychologist make a report of suspected child abuse?

- **A)** Within 48 hours from the time there is reasonable cause to believe suspected child abuse occurred
- **B)** Within 72 hours from the time there is reasonable cause to believe suspected child abuse occurred
- **C)** Within 7 calendar days, followed by a formal notarized report within 30 days
- **D)** Immediately, but in no case later than 24 hours from the time there is reasonable cause to believe suspected child abuse occurred

### Q39. What must psychologists base assessment opinions on?

- **A)** Information and techniques sufficient to substantiate findings
- **B)** Statistical averages of the demographic group without individualized testing
- **C)** Testimonials and collateral reports from interested third-party family members
- **D)** Intuitive clinical impressions gathered during an informal initial meeting

### Q40. May a Georgia psychologist supervise someone who surrendered a license due to discipline?

- **A)** Yes, provided the supervisee practices exclusively in private outpatient clinics
- **B)** Yes, automatically after a six-month waiting period
- **C)** Yes, if the supervisee signs an agreement not to bill commercial insurance
- **D)** No, unless Board waives/modifies for extraordinary circumstances

### Q41. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 6.02 (Rule 510-4-.02), what precaution must psychologists take when entering confidential client records into databases accessible to non-consenting persons?

- **A)** Require the database administrator to obtain an active Georgia psychology license before access is granted
- **B)** File a copy of all electronic database credentials with the Georgia Board of Examiners of Psychologists
- **C)** Use coding or other de-identification techniques to avoid the inclusion of personal identifiers
- **D)** Obtain a court subpoena before uploading any diagnostic codes into electronic health record systems

### Q42. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), what is required before recording the voices or images of clients receiving psychological services?

- **A)** Ensuring the recording is stored on an unencrypted removable USB flash drive for seven years
- **B)** Obtaining permission from all clients or their legal representatives prior to recording
- **C)** Providing verbal notice at least twenty-four hours after the recorded clinical session has concluded
- **D)** Obtaining approval from the Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists ethics committee

### Q43. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 5.02 (Rule 510-4-.02), who retains professional responsibility for public statements created or placed by others to promote a psychologist's practice?

- **A)** The media publisher or platform host that broadcast or printed the promotional material
- **B)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists upon approving the promotional copy
- **C)** The marketing firm or public relations agency that authored and published the advertisement
- **D)** The psychologist retains full professional responsibility for all such promotional statements

### Q44. Under O.C.G.A. § 30-5-4(b)(2), by what means may a mandated report of suspected disabled adult or elder abuse be submitted?

- **A)** By oral or written communication
- **B)** Exclusively by certified mail with return receipt requested
- **C)** Exclusively by formal verified petition filed in the probate court
- **D)** Exclusively by in-person personal appearance at the county adult protection office

### Q45. Under OCGA 16-13-110, what dispositions are included within the definition of 'convicted' for licensed-person drug sanctions?

- **A)** Final conviction, plea of guilty or nolo contendere, or first offender treatment
- **B)** Any arrest for a felony drug offense resulting in formal booking and cash bail
- **C)** Entry of formal criminal indictment or accusation by a county prosecuting attorney
- **D)** Final conviction following exhaustion of all state and federal appellate reviews

### Q46. After two years, who bears the burden of showing no exploitation with a former client sexual relationship?

- **A)** The psychologist
- **B)** The former client who alleges exploitation
- **C)** The American Psychological Association Ethics Committee
- **D)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners

### Q47. Who must make the diagnosis when clinical services are rendered by a supervisee?

- **A)** The supervising psychologist personally
- **B)** The client's primary care physician
- **C)** The supervisee, with post-hoc cosignature on billing invoices
- **D)** A consensus panel of practicum students

### Q48. Who should be principal author on a dissertation-based article?

- **A)** The student, except under exceptional circumstances
- **B)** The dissertation faculty advisor who supervised the research design
- **C)** Authorship must always be listed strictly alphabetically regardless of contribution
- **D)** The department chairperson who secured grant funding for the laboratory

### Q49. Under Rule 510-5-.07, what emergency resources must telepsychology practitioners identify and learn how to access?

- **A)** The immediate supervisor or designated colleague on call at the psychologist's clinic
- **B)** National crisis call centers without identifying any local emergency responders
- **C)** The emergency department closest to the psychologist's primary physical office location
- **D)** Relevant and appropriate emergency resources in the client's local geographic area

### Q50. May psychologists have sexual intimacies with close relatives/significant others of current clients?

- **A)** Yes, if the relative resides in a different household from the client
- **B)** Yes, because relatives are not direct clients of the psychologist
- **C)** No; psychologists may not engage in sexual intimacies with individuals known to be close relatives or significant others of current clients
- **D)** Yes, provided the client gives written permission
