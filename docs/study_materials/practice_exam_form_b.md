## Section 1: Examination Questions

### Q1. Under Board Rule 510-8-.02, how many continuing education credits may be earned for teaching a semester-long psychology course for the first time?

- **A)** A maximum of 10 CE credits per course, up to 20 CE credits per biennium
- **B)** A maximum of 5 CE credits per course, up to 10 CE credits per biennium
- **C)** A maximum of 20 CE credits per course, up to 40 CE credits per biennium
- **D)** A maximum of 15 CE credits per course, up to 30 CE credits per biennium

### Q2. Under Board Rule 510-9-.01, who is eligible to apply for a temporary psychology license in Georgia?

- **A)** Recent doctoral graduates who are currently completing their predoctoral internships
- **B)** Applicants who have submitted a completed application for licensure by endorsement
- **C)** Licensure candidates completing their postdoctoral supervised work experience
- **D)** Out-of-state psychologists seeking uncompensated volunteer practice in disaster areas

### Q3. Under Board Rule 510-9-.04, what continuing education is required to obtain a license for volunteer service?

- **A)** 24 hours in the two years immediately prior to application
- **B)** 40 hours in the two years immediately prior to application
- **C)** 20 hours in the two years immediately prior to application
- **D)** 12 hours in the two years immediately prior to application

### Q4. Under Board Rule 510-5-.02, which tax reporting form generally reflects an employer-employee relationship for clinical personnel?

- **A)** IRS Form 1040 Schedule C (Profit or Loss from Business)
- **B)** IRS Form W-2 (Wage and Tax Statement)
- **C)** IRS Form 990 (Return of Organization Exempt From Income Tax)
- **D)** IRS Form 1099-NEC (Nonemployee Compensation)

### Q5. What is 'child abuse' under Georgia law?

- **A)** Normal childhood conflicts between siblings that cause emotional distress
- **B)** Physical injury or death inflicted by a parent/custodian/other caretaker
- **C)** Truancy or willful refusal to attend public school without parental consent
- **D)** Reasonable corporal punishment administered by a parent or legal guardian

### Q6. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.09 (Rule 510-4-.02), what is a psychologist's ethical responsibility when cooperation with other professionals is indicated?

- **A)** Cooperate with other professionals in order to serve their clients/patients effectively and appropriately
- **B)** Demand sole decision-making authority and refuse to share any clinical treatment summaries
- **C)** Condition cooperation on the other professional adopting the psychologist's theoretical orientation
- **D)** Require the client to terminate all contact with other medical providers before initiating therapy

### Q7. Under O.C.G.A. § 30-5-4(b)(2), by what means may a mandated report of suspected disabled adult or elder abuse be submitted?

- **A)** Exclusively by formal verified petition filed in the probate court
- **B)** By oral or written communication
- **C)** Exclusively by in-person personal appearance at the county adult protection office
- **D)** Exclusively by certified mail with return receipt requested

### Q8. When must psychologists discuss limits of confidentiality?

- **A)** Only if the client explicitly inquires about confidentiality policies
- **B)** At the outset of the relationship and thereafter as circumstances warrant
- **C)** Exclusively at the termination session when closing the clinical chart
- **D)** Only after a breach of confidentiality or legal subpoena has actually occurred

### Q9. What other professionals' communications are privileged under OCGA 24-9-21?

- **A)** Psychiatrist-patient; licensed clinical social worker, clinical nurse specialist psych/mental health, marriage and family therapist, professional counselor during psychotherapeutic relationship
- **B)** Physical therapists, occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, and medical assistants
- **C)** Registered nurses, hospital administrators, medical records clerks, and case managers
- **D)** Attorneys, clergy, and certified public accountants only

### Q10. Under O.C.G.A. § 30-5-4(d), does statutory or professional privilege prevent mandatory reporting of elder or disabled adult abuse?

- **A)** No; abuse, neglect, or exploitation must be reported notwithstanding any privileged communication (except clergy confession)
- **B)** Yes; communications between a psychologist and an adult client are completely confidential and immune from reporting
- **C)** Yes; reporting is prohibited unless the disabled adult or elder person provides signed informed consent to report
- **D)** No; however, reporting is excused if the psychologist determines that reporting would harm therapeutic rapport

### Q11. Under Board Rule 510-2-.05, may a psychologist serve as primary postdoctoral supervisor if they are employed by an entity in which the supervisee has an ownership interest?

- **A)** Yes; provided the supervisee's ownership share in the clinical entity is less than 50%
- **B)** Yes; provided secondary supervision is completed by an independent licensed psychologist
- **C)** No; a supervisor cannot be employed by an entity in which the supervisee holds a proprietary or ownership interest
- **D)** No; unless the supervisory relationship is registered with the Secretary of State Corporations Division

### Q12. Under Board Rule 510-8-.02, how many continuing education credits may be earned for first or second authoring of a peer-reviewed journal article?

- **A)** A maximum of 10 CE credits per article, up to a total of 20 CE credits per biennium
- **B)** A maximum of 15 CE credits per article, up to a total of 30 CE credits per biennium
- **C)** A maximum of 5 CE credits per article, up to a total of 10 CE credits per biennium
- **D)** A maximum of 3 CE credits per article, up to a total of 6 CE credits per biennium

### Q13. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.11(b) (Rule 510-4-.02), what must psychologists do if organizational roles or law preclude providing feedback or disclosure to individuals?

- **A)** Inform the affected individuals and groups of that restriction at the outset of the service
- **B)** File a formal ethics complaint against the organization with the Georgia Department of Labor
- **C)** Withhold all evaluation findings from both the individuals and the organizational leadership
- **D)** Disclose the confidential findings secretly to the individuals despite organizational agreements

### Q14. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 10.07 (Rule 510-4-.02), may psychologists accept as therapy clients persons with whom they have engaged in sexual intimacies?

- **A)** No; psychologists do not accept as therapy clients persons with whom they have engaged in sexual intimacies
- **B)** Yes; provided at least two years have elapsed since the prior sexual relationship terminated
- **C)** Yes; provided another licensed psychologist conducts concurrent monthly supervision
- **D)** No; unless the client signs a waiver confirming that the prior sexual relationship was consensual

### Q15. Under Board Rule 510-8-.02, if a psychologist is licensed during the second year of the renewal biennium, how many continuing education credits are required for the first renewal?

- **A)** 20 credits, including 3 ethics credits
- **B)** Zero continuing education credits are required for the first renewal cycle
- **C)** 6 credits in professional ethics only
- **D)** 10 credits, with no ethics requirement

### Q16. Under Board Rule 510-2-.04, are substantially or completely online doctoral programs acceptable to fulfill the continuous academic residency requirement?

- **A)** No; unless the distance learning program was accredited by the APA prior to January 1, 2017
- **B)** Yes; provided students attend at least two weekend in-person intensive colloquia per academic year
- **C)** Yes; online synchronous doctoral programs satisfy all residency requirements under ASPPB standards
- **D)** No; Georgia residency obligations cannot be met in programs that are substantially or completely online

### Q17. Under Board Rule 510-9-.02, who is eligible to be granted a provisional psychology license in Georgia?

- **A)** A psychologist whose regular license has lapsed and who has applied for Board reinstatement
- **B)** An out-of-state licensed psychologist waiting for endorsement transcripts to arrive at the Board
- **C)** A graduate student currently enrolled in an APA-accredited predoctoral internship program
- **D)** An individual who has passed all written examinations and completed all requirements except the postdoctoral supervised work experience and oral examination

### Q18. What must records include?

- **A)** Complete transcripts of all spoken dialogue and personal feelings of the therapist
- **B)** Photographs of the client and personal contact details of all extended family members
- **C)** Client personal tax returns and detailed credit card account histories
- **D)** Identifying/contact data, fees/billing, informed consent, diagnosis/basis, plan, dates/services, interventions, assessment

### Q19. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), what conduct is prohibited regarding unfair discrimination and harassment?

- **A)** Selecting specialized psychological assessment batteries based on the client's primary language
- **B)** Charging different sliding-scale hourly professional fees based on verified household income levels
- **C)** Referring clients with complex medical comorbidities to board-certified medical specialists
- **D)** Unfair discrimination and harassment or demeaning conduct based on protected personal characteristics

### Q20. What must reports to payers accurately reflect?

- **A)** Detailed psychotherapy process transcripts and client personal diary entries
- **B)** Generalized medical codes that conceal mental health diagnoses
- **C)** Nature of service, fees, provider identity, findings, diagnosis
- **D)** Any billing code that maximizes insurance reimbursement for the client

### Q21. Under Board Rule 510-2-.05, which licensure fellows are exempt from the standard 1-hour-per-30-hours SWE supervision ratio?

- **A)** Industrial-Organizational (I/O) psychology fellows
- **B)** Forensic psychology postdoctoral fellows
- **C)** Clinical neuropsychology postdoctoral fellows
- **D)** School psychology postdoctoral fellows

### Q22. Under O.C.G.A. § 19-7-5(e), to which agencies must a mandated reporter make a report of suspected child abuse?

- **A)** The juvenile court clerk of the county where the psychologist maintains their primary clinical office
- **B)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists or the Georgia Composite Medical Board
- **C)** The United States Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights
- **D)** The child welfare agency (DFCS) of the county where the child resides or abuse occurred, or an appropriate police authority or district attorney

### Q23. What PSYPACT credentials allow interjurisdictional telepsychology?

- **A)** ABPP Specialty Diploma and National Register Health Service Psychologist credential
- **B)** Certificate of Professional Qualification (CPQ) and Georgia temporary license
- **C)** ASPPB Interjurisdictional Practice Certificate (IPC) and TAP authorization
- **D)** ASPPB E.Passport and APIT authorization

### Q24. Under Board Rule 510-3-.02, which national examination parts are required for endorsement applicants who were originally licensed on or after November 1, 2020?

- **A)** EPPP Part 1 (Knowledge) only, as Part 2 is entirely optional for all endorsement candidates
- **B)** Both EPPP Part 1 (Knowledge) and Part 2 (Skills)
- **C)** The ABPP specialty board examination in lieu of any national written examinations
- **D)** The GRE Subject Test in Psychology and the Miller Analogies Test

### Q25. How long after lapse may a Georgia psychology license be reinstated without re-examination?

- **A)** Within one year
- **B)** Within six months
- **C)** Within three years
- **D)** Within two years

### Q26. What does 'test materials' include?

- **A)** Manuals, instruments, protocols, and test questions/stimuli; excludes test data
- **B)** Raw and scaled scores, client test answer sheets, and clinical interview notes
- **C)** Billing statements, superbills, and insurance explanation of benefits forms
- **D)** Psychotherapy process notes and audio recordings of clinical intake sessions

### Q27. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 5.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), what elements must announcements describing non-degree-granting workshops or seminars accurately describe?

- **A)** Sponsoring university endowments, commercial vendor sponsorships, and profit margins
- **B)** State licensure exam pass rates, average salary outcomes, and placement rates
- **C)** Individual attendees' clinical background, board exam scores, and malpractice history
- **D)** Intended audience, educational objectives, presenters, and fees involved

### Q28. What is 'psychotherapy' under OCGA 16-6-5.1?

- **A)** Academic instruction and tutoring in psychology delivered in university settings
- **B)** Professional treatment or counseling of a mental or emotional illness, symptom, or condition
- **C)** Career counseling and vocational interest inventory administration
- **D)** General life coaching and organizational consulting for corporate executives

### Q29. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 9.09 (Rule 510-4-.02), who retains ultimate responsibility for the appropriate application, interpretation, and use of psychological assessment instruments when automated scoring services are used?

- **A)** The psychologist who selects and uses the assessment instrument or scoring service
- **B)** The clerical staff member or psychometrist who entered raw response data into the computer
- **C)** The commercial software developer or test publisher that programmed the scoring algorithm
- **D)** The licensing board or accrediting body that approved the continuing education assessment workshop

### Q30. Under Board Rule 510-5-.02, how is a 'professional relationship' defined between a psychologist and a recipient of services?

- **A)** Any casual social conversation occurring between a psychologist and a member of the public
- **B)** Any commercial contract where money is exchanged regardless of mental health service provision
- **C)** A relationship that exists only after a minimum of ten consecutive therapy sessions have been completed
- **D)** A mutually agreed upon relationship between a psychologist and a patient, client, student, supervisee, or examinee

### Q31. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-1-21, may the division director disclose information regarding past or pending investigations to other licensing authorities?

- **A)** No; investigative information is strictly confidential and cannot be disclosed before a formal consent order is executed
- **B)** No; disclosure to out-of-state licensing authorities requires a formal federal grand jury subpoena
- **C)** Yes; the division director is authorized to provide investigative and disciplinary information to any lawful licensing authority upon inquiry
- **D)** Yes; but only if the investigated licensee executes a notarized consent form authorizing the disclosure

### Q32. What standard governs court-appointed custody psychologists' good faith?

- **A)** APA's 2010 Guidelines for Child Custody Evaluations in Family Law Proceedings
- **B)** Georgia Rules of Professional Conduct for Attorneys
- **C)** Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-5)
- **D)** Federal Rules of Evidence Rule 702

### Q33. Under Board Rule 510-10-.03, under what legal conditions may psychology students or trainees administer and interpret psychological tests?

- **A)** Only when administering standardized objective tests that do not involve projective techniques or clinical interviews
- **B)** Only when they have completed at least two years of doctoral coursework and hold written authorization from ASPPB
- **C)** Without supervision, provided the test scores are reviewed and co-signed by an agency administrative director
- **D)** When they satisfy requisite statutory and rule requirements and practice under direct supervision of a licensed psychologist

### Q34. Under Board Rule 510-8-.02, what is the maximum number of continuing education credits per biennium that may be earned through Area II group consultation?

- **A)** 15 CE credits per biennium
- **B)** 20 CE credits per biennium
- **C)** 30 CE credits per biennium
- **D)** 10 CE credits per biennium

### Q35. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-8, what is required before any person may engage in the practice of psychology in Georgia?

- **A)** Submit a formal application to the Board, meet statutory qualifications, and obtain a valid license
- **B)** Register a clinical business trade name with the county superior court clerk only
- **C)** File a surety bond of $100,000 with the Office of the Georgia Secretary of State
- **D)** Obtain a diploma from an accredited doctoral psychology program without applying to the Board

### Q36. Under Board Rule 510-2-.01, how is a Full Board oral examination defined?

- **A)** An oral examination administered jointly by the Georgia Board and the ASPPB Board of Directors
- **B)** An oral examination conducted by all seven Board members with unanimous approval required to pass
- **C)** An oral examination conducted by the Division Director and an administrative law judge
- **D)** An oral examination in which the majority of Board members are present

### Q37. Who determines whether a court-appointed custody psychologist acted in bad faith?

- **A)** The American Psychological Association Ethics Committee
- **B)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists
- **C)** The complaining parent
- **D)** The presiding judge of the court making the appointment

### Q38. What privilege applies to psychologist-client communications in Georgia?

- **A)** Qualified peer review immunity under hospital regulations
- **B)** Same as attorney-client privilege
- **C)** Physician-patient privilege under common law
- **D)** Work-product doctrine applicable to civil litigation

### Q39. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.02 (Rule 510-4-.02), when must psychologists discuss the relevant limits of confidentiality with clients?

- **A)** Exclusively at the final termination session after all psychological services have concluded
- **B)** Only if the client formally requests written information concerning privacy policies
- **C)** Within 60 calendar days following the submission of the initial diagnostic insurance claim
- **D)** At the outset of the relationship and thereafter as new circumstances may warrant, unless not feasible or contraindicated

### Q40. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-3(a), may a criminal conviction for statutory rape be obtained solely on the uncorroborated testimony of the alleged victim?

- **A)** Yes; Georgia evidentiary law permits conviction based entirely on the uncorroborated testimony of any victim of a sexual offense
- **B)** Yes; provided the victim's testimony is delivered under oath in open court and deemed credible by the jury
- **C)** No; Georgia statutory law explicitly mandates that no conviction for statutory rape may be had on the uncorroborated testimony of the victim alone
- **D)** No; conviction requires two independent eyewitnesses to the act of intercourse in addition to physical medical evidence

### Q41. What CPQ holders are deemed to meet Georgia licensure requirements?

- **A)** CPQ holders must complete 1,500 additional hours of postdoctoral Georgia supervision
- **B)** CPQ holders must re-take and pass the national EPPP examination at the current pass point
- **C)** ASPPB CPQ holders are granted immediate active Georgia licensure without examination
- **D)** ASPPB Certificate of Professional Qualification holders who pass jurisprudence and oral exams

### Q42. Under Rule 510-5-.07, what emergency resources must telepsychology practitioners identify and learn how to access?

- **A)** National crisis call centers without identifying any local emergency responders
- **B)** The emergency department closest to the psychologist's primary physical office location
- **C)** Relevant and appropriate emergency resources in the client's local geographic area
- **D)** The immediate supervisor or designated colleague on call at the psychologist's clinic

### Q43. What must the SWE weekly log contain?

- **A)** Detailed psychotherapy process notes, treatment transcripts, and fee ledgers
- **B)** Billing codes, client insurance records, and DSM-5 diagnostic formulations
- **C)** Activities, total hours, client/patient involvement hours, and individual supervision hours
- **D)** Supervisor personal evaluations, audio recordings of sessions, and client outcome metrics

### Q44. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-1(7), can a psychologist's description of testing as 'psychological' in nature alter the statutory definition of psychological testing?

- **A)** Yes; any assessment labeled by a licensed psychologist as psychological testing automatically becomes psychological testing by law
- **B)** No; unless the psychologist provides written notice to the Georgia Composite Board forty-eight hours prior to evaluation
- **C)** Yes; describing educational tests as psychological expands the psychologist's exclusive statutory testing domain
- **D)** No; statutory law provides that such descriptions shall not be construed to alter the definition of psychological testing

### Q45. Where is an unlicensed practice injunction filed?

- **A)** County where the person resides or where corporation maintains principal office
- **B)** The Georgia Court of Appeals by direct original petition
- **C)** Superior Court of Fulton County exclusively in all instances
- **D)** The municipal court of the city where the unauthorized service occurred

### Q46. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.11(a) (Rule 510-4-.02), what information must psychologists provide at the outset when delivering services to or through organizations?

- **A)** Guaranteed promises that organizational data will be withheld from corporate management under attorney-client privilege
- **B)** The personal credit history and private medical records of corporate executives and board members
- **C)** The proprietary computer source code and psychometric formulas of all organizational assessment tests
- **D)** Nature/objectives of services, intended recipients, client identity, relationship to each party, uses of info, and limits of confidentiality

### Q47. Who may bring an injunction for unlicensed practice?

- **A)** The Georgia Psychological Association
- **B)** Any aggrieved consumer or client
- **C)** The Georgia Department of Public Health
- **D)** The Board

### Q48. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.10(c) (Rule 510-4-.02), what must psychologists disclose to individuals when psychological services are court ordered or mandated?

- **A)** That communications are protected by absolute privilege and can never be disclosed to the judge
- **B)** The nature of the anticipated services, that services are court ordered or mandated, and any limits of confidentiality
- **C)** That the individual has an unconditional right to prevent the psychologist from submitting an evaluation report
- **D)** That the psychologist serves as the individual's personal legal defense advocate during the proceedings

### Q49. Under OCGA 19-7-5, within what period must a parent come forward after a child is found without identification to avoid a finding of abandonment?

- **A)** One calendar year
- **B)** Three months
- **C)** 30 calendar days
- **D)** Six months

### Q50. What is the plagiarism rule?

- **A)** Do not present another's work or data as one's own, even if cited
- **B)** Presenting another's work is permitted if paraphrased without citation
- **C)** Plagiarism rules apply only to published books and not journal articles or dissertations
- **D)** Borrowing up to 25% of another's text without attribution is considered fair use
