## Exam Instructions & Scoring Guide

- **Time Limit:** 60 minutes (strict simulation conditions)
- **Item Count:** 50 multiple-choice questions (A, B, C, D)
- **Passing Standard:** 38 out of 50 correct (76.0%)
- **Format:** Closed-book examination. Select the single best answer for each question.
- **Official Domain Distribution:**
  - Professional Relationships: 14 questions (28%)
  - Licensure and Regulations: 13 questions (26%)
  - Competence: 9 questions (18%)
  - Assessment: 6 questions (12%)
  - Privacy and Confidentiality: 5 questions (10%)

## Section 1: Examination Questions

### Q1. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), what conduct is prohibited regarding unfair discrimination and harassment?

- **A)** Unfair discrimination and harassment or demeaning conduct based on protected personal characteristics
- **B)** Referring clients with complex medical comorbidities to board-certified medical specialists
- **C)** Selecting specialized psychological assessment batteries based on the client's primary language
- **D)** Charging different sliding-scale hourly professional fees based on verified household income levels

### Q2. How many interns/fellows may a supervisor oversee concurrently without Board approval?

- **A)** No more than five
- **B)** No more than three
- **C)** No more than four
- **D)** No more than two

### Q3. How many supervisees may a psychologist supervise concurrently without Board approval?

- **A)** No more than three
- **B)** No more than one
- **C)** No more than five
- **D)** No more than two

### Q4. Under Rule 510-5-.08, what must forensic assessments, recommendations, and reports be based on?

- **A)** Polygraph examination results combined with character references submitted by family members
- **B)** Actuarial recidivism instruments administered without any individual collateral record review
- **C)** Subjective impressions and secondhand hearsay reported by retaining litigation attorneys
- **D)** Information and techniques sufficient to substantiate findings, including personal interviews when appropriate

### Q5. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-7(10), which agency has sole regulatory authority over testing and assessment performed by licensed professional counselors and social workers?

- **A)** The Georgia Composite Medical Board
- **B)** The Professional Standards Commission of the Georgia Department of Education
- **C)** The Georgia Composite Board of Professional Counselors, Social Workers, and Marriage and Family Therapists
- **D)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists

### Q6. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-2 and § 43-39-18, what immunity do Georgia psychology board members possess for official actions?

- **A)** Immunity from civil liability for acts performed in good faith in official duties
- **B)** Qualified immunity from criminal prosecution only, with full civil tort liability
- **C)** Limited immunity that applies only if the Attorney General pre-approves each regulatory decision
- **D)** Absolute immunity covering intentional torts and gross negligence in the course of proceedings

### Q7. Under Board Rule 510-5-.10, may a Georgia psychologist employ or supervise an individual who has voluntarily surrendered a psychology license under disciplinary proceedings?

- **A)** No; the Board is statutorily prohibited from granting an extraordinary circumstances waiver under any conditions
- **B)** No; supervision or employment in any capacity is prohibited unless the Board waives or modifies this restriction for extraordinary circumstances
- **C)** Yes; employment is permissible automatically after a mandatory two-year waiting period
- **D)** Yes; provided the supervisee practices exclusively in private outpatient clinics and signs a supervision agreement

### Q8. Under Board Rule 510-5-.02, how is a 'professional relationship' defined between a psychologist and a recipient of services?

- **A)** Any commercial contract where money is exchanged regardless of mental health service provision
- **B)** Any casual social conversation occurring between a psychologist and a member of the public
- **C)** A mutually agreed upon relationship between a psychologist and a patient, client, student, supervisee, or examinee
- **D)** A relationship that exists only after a minimum of ten consecutive therapy sessions have been completed

### Q9. What must assessment instruments have for the population tested?

- **A)** Established validity and reliability; if not, describe limitations
- **B)** Universal cultural applicability without need for normative adaptations
- **C)** Formal endorsement and certification by the Georgia Department of Education
- **D)** Public-domain status allowing unrestricted photocopying of test stimuli

### Q10. Which of the following is a statutory ground for license revocation under OCGA 43-39-13?

- **A)** Refusing to provide pro bono psychological services to indigent clients annually
- **B)** Practicing under a false or assumed name
- **C)** Charging fees exceeding standard Medicare or commercial insurance reimbursement rates
- **D)** Failure to join or maintain membership in the Georgia Psychological Association

### Q11. Under Board Rule 510-8-.02, how many continuing education credits may be earned for first or second authoring of a peer-reviewed journal article?

- **A)** A maximum of 10 CE credits per article, up to a total of 20 CE credits per biennium
- **B)** A maximum of 15 CE credits per article, up to a total of 30 CE credits per biennium
- **C)** A maximum of 5 CE credits per article, up to a total of 10 CE credits per biennium
- **D)** A maximum of 3 CE credits per article, up to a total of 6 CE credits per biennium

### Q12. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-1-19, may a licensing board refuse to grant a license to an applicant without a prior administrative hearing?

- **A)** Yes; the board may refuse to grant a license without a prior hearing if not satisfied with qualifications, but the applicant may request an appearance
- **B)** Yes; the board may deny any license arbitrarily without providing any statutory basis or opportunity to be heard
- **C)** No; an applicant is automatically granted a license if the board fails to hold a formal hearing within 10 days
- **D)** No; the Georgia Administrative Procedure Act requires a full jury trial prior to any initial license denial

### Q13. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 9.07 (Rule 510-4-.02), may psychologists promote the use of psychological assessment techniques by unqualified persons?

- **A)** Yes; whenever unqualified persons purchase automated computer interpretation software
- **B)** No; unless conducted for training purposes under appropriate professional supervision
- **C)** No; unqualified persons are prohibited from administering tests even during closely supervised clinical training
- **D)** Yes; to reduce assessment backlogs in public community mental health centers

### Q14. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 6.04 (Rule 510-4-.02), what must psychologists do before using collection agencies or legal measures to collect unpaid client fees?

- **A)** Immediately disclose the client's complete psychotherapeutic session notes to the credit reporting bureau
- **B)** Obtain written authorization from the Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists
- **C)** File a formal grievance against the client with the American Psychological Association ethics committee
- **D)** First inform the person that such measures will be taken and provide an opportunity for prompt payment

### Q15. According to the PSI Candidate Information Bulletin, what is the official testing time allowance for the Georgia Psychology Jurisprudence Examination?

- **A)** 45 minutes
- **B)** 120 minutes (2 hours)
- **C)** 60 minutes (1 hour)
- **D)** 90 minutes (1.5 hours)

### Q16. What other professionals' communications are privileged under OCGA 24-9-21?

- **A)** Registered nurses, hospital administrators, medical records clerks, and case managers
- **B)** Psychiatrist-patient; licensed clinical social worker, clinical nurse specialist psych/mental health, marriage and family therapist, professional counselor during psychotherapeutic relationship
- **C)** Physical therapists, occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, and medical assistants
- **D)** Attorneys, clergy, and certified public accountants only

### Q17. Under Board Rule 510-3-.02, which national examination parts are required for endorsement applicants who were originally licensed on or after November 1, 2020?

- **A)** EPPP Part 1 (Knowledge) only, as Part 2 is entirely optional for all endorsement candidates
- **B)** Both EPPP Part 1 (Knowledge) and Part 2 (Skills)
- **C)** The GRE Subject Test in Psychology and the Miller Analogies Test
- **D)** The ABPP specialty board examination in lieu of any national written examinations

### Q18. What must psychologists clarify at the outset of third-party-requested services?

- **A)** The specific billing codes to be submitted to commercial health insurance
- **B)** The complete psychometric test battery to be administered during assessment
- **C)** Role, who is the client, probable uses of information, and limits of confidentiality
- **D)** The identity of all expert witnesses who may testify in subsequent litigation

### Q19. To whom must reports of disabled adult/elder abuse be made?

- **A)** Adult protection agency and appropriate law enforcement or prosecuting attorney
- **B)** Exclusively to the Georgia composite medical board within 24 hours
- **C)** The Department of Community Health and the Secretary of State
- **D)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists

### Q20. Under O.C.G.A. § 30-5-4, are individuals who report suspected abuse of disabled adults or elder persons immune from liability?

- **A)** No; immunity is granted only to licensed physicians and hospital administrators
- **B)** Yes; but immunity applies only to civil damages, leaving reporters subject to criminal misdemeanor prosecution
- **C)** Yes; any person who in good faith makes a report has immunity from any civil or criminal liability
- **D)** No; reporters may be sued for defamation if the investigation substantiates no abuse

### Q21. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 10.06 (Rule 510-4-.02), may psychologists engage in sexual intimacies with close relatives, guardians, or significant others of current clients?

- **A)** Yes; because relatives and significant others are not direct clients of the psychologist
- **B)** Yes; provided the client gives prior written permission and therapy is temporarily paused
- **C)** No; unless the relative or significant other resides in a different household from the client
- **D)** No; psychologists are prohibited from sexual intimacies with known close relatives, guardians, or significant others of current clients

### Q22. Under Rule 510-2-.04, how many in-person coursework hours are required to demonstrate residency equivalency?

- **A)** Minimum 1,000 in-person coursework hours
- **B)** Minimum 750 in-person coursework hours
- **C)** Minimum 540 in-person coursework hours
- **D)** Minimum 250 in-person coursework hours

### Q23. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-15, under what conditions may the Board waive continuing education requirements for a licensee?

- **A)** Whenever the licensee has practiced psychology in Georgia for at least five consecutive years
- **B)** The Board has no statutory authority to waive continuing education under any circumstances
- **C)** Whenever the licensee earns more than $150,000 in gross annual clinical billing receipts
- **D)** In cases of hardship, disability, illness, or other appropriate circumstances demonstrated to the Board

### Q24. Under Board Rule 510-8-.02, if a psychologist is licensed during the second year of the renewal biennium, how many continuing education credits are required for the first renewal?

- **A)** 6 credits in professional ethics only
- **B)** 20 credits, including 3 ethics credits
- **C)** Zero continuing education credits are required for the first renewal cycle
- **D)** 10 credits, with no ethics requirement

### Q25. Under Board Rule 510-2-.01, what must an applicant complete before retaking the examination after three unsuccessful EPPP attempts?

- **A)** Retake and pass the Georgia Jurisprudence Examination before any subsequent EPPP attempt
- **B)** Earn in person a minimum of 3 semester hours of Board-approved graduate coursework or 15 hours of approved CE
- **C)** Complete a new 1,500-hour postdoctoral supervised work experience under a new primary supervisor
- **D)** Earn in person a minimum of 9 semester hours of graduate coursework or 45 hours of approved CE

### Q26. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-7(9), does a psychologist practicing under the authority of PSYPACT need to obtain a standard Georgia license?

- **A)** Yes; PSYPACT practitioners must complete the Georgia Jurisprudence Examination and obtain a full Georgia license
- **B)** No; but PSYPACT practitioners must maintain a full-time physical clinical office located within the State of Georgia
- **C)** No; practicing pursuant to the Psychology Interjurisdictional Compact (PSYPACT) is an express statutory exception to Georgia licensure
- **D)** Yes; PSYPACT practitioners must submit an endorsement application to the Board within thirty days of initiating practice

### Q27. What is 'test data' under the APA Ethics Code?

- **A)** Raw and scaled scores, client responses, and psychologist notes/recordings during examination
- **B)** Published psychometric test manuals, test protocols, stimuli, and test questions
- **C)** Billing codes, clinical appointment logs, and fee payment receipts
- **D)** Peer-reviewed validation studies evaluating norm samples and reliability coefficients

### Q28. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 6.05 (Rule 510-4-.02), when is bartering for psychological services ethically permissible?

- **A)** Under no circumstances, as bartering is strictly prohibited in Georgia psychological practice
- **B)** Only when approved in advance by a formal written consent order issued by the Georgia Board
- **C)** Only when the fair market value of the bartered goods or services does not exceed fifty dollars
- **D)** Only if it is not clinically contraindicated and the resulting arrangement is not exploitative

### Q29. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-5.1(d), is client consent an admissible defense to criminal prosecution for improper sexual contact by a psychotherapist?

- **A)** No; consent of the victim shall not be a defense to criminal prosecution under this statute
- **B)** Yes; documented informed consent signed prior to the sexual contact serves as a complete affirmative defense
- **C)** No; unless the psychotherapeutic relationship was formally terminated in writing at least six months prior
- **D)** Yes; client consent is an absolute defense provided both parties are competent adults over age twenty-one

### Q30. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 2.06(a) (Rule 510-4-.02), what must psychologists refrain from doing when their personal problems may impair work performance?

- **A)** Refrain from providing psychotherapy to adults while continuing to conduct juvenile evaluations
- **B)** Refrain from seeking independent psychiatric treatment or medical care for the personal problems
- **C)** Refrain from billing third-party insurance payors while continuing regular therapy sessions
- **D)** Refrain from initiating activities when there is a substantial likelihood personal problems will prevent competent performance

### Q31. What happens if an impaired licensee refuses evaluation/rehabilitation?

- **A)** The licensee is granted an automatic 12-month leave of absence
- **B)** May constitute basis for disciplinary sanctions
- **C)** The matter is referred to the APA Ethics Committee for binding arbitration
- **D)** The Board must dismiss all pending complaints with prejudice

### Q32. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-5.1(f), what is the statutory penalty for improper sexual contact by a psychotherapist in the first degree?

- **A)** Imprisonment for 1 to 5 years and a fine not to exceed $10,000 (5 to 10 years if the victim is under 16)
- **B)** Imprisonment for 5 to 10 years and a fine not to exceed $25,000 (10 to 20 years if the victim is under 16)
- **C)** Imprisonment for 1 to 25 years and a fine not to exceed $100,000 (10 to 30 years if the victim is under 16)
- **D)** Imprisonment for 1 to 10 years and a fine not to exceed $50,000 (10 to 25 years if the victim is under 16)

### Q33. To whom may test data be released without a client release?

- **A)** Only as required by law or court order
- **B)** Any family member who pays for the psychological evaluation
- **C)** Any licensed healthcare professional requesting records by telephone
- **D)** The client's employer upon submission of a formal workplace inquiry

### Q34. Under OCGA 30-5-4, if a mandated reporter of disabled adult or elder abuse is on the staff of a hospital or facility, who makes the report?

- **A)** The staff member must directly contact law enforcement without notifying facility administrators
- **B)** The staff member notifies the person in charge or designee, who then reports or causes the report to be made
- **C)** The facility's board of directors must convene a formal meeting to vote on making a report
- **D)** The facility's risk management legal counsel evaluates the incident and decides whether to report

### Q35. What is the plagiarism rule?

- **A)** Presenting another's work is permitted if paraphrased without citation
- **B)** Plagiarism rules apply only to published books and not journal articles or dissertations
- **C)** Borrowing up to 25% of another's text without attribution is considered fair use
- **D)** Do not present another's work or data as one's own, even if cited

### Q36. What must psychologists do with records after termination?

- **A)** Discard records immediately upon termination to protect client privacy
- **B)** Publicly auction closed files to recover uncollected co-payments
- **C)** Transfer all clinical files to the Georgia Psychological Association archives
- **D)** Continue to treat client information as confidential

### Q37. What is required to practice telepsychology in Georgia?

- **A)** Current active Georgia license or PSYPACT authorization
- **B)** A national telebehavioral health certificate with no state license
- **C)** Registration with the Federal Communications Commission (FCC)
- **D)** A valid license in any U.S. state without notification or PSYPACT authority

### Q38. What standard governs disclosures of confidential information without consent?

- **A)** Disclosures without consent are strictly prohibited under every statutory provision
- **B)** Disclosures are permitted whenever advantageous to the psychologist's academic research
- **C)** Only as mandated by law or permitted by law for valid purposes, limited to the minimum necessary
- **D)** Psychologists may disclose confidential information whenever requested by family members

### Q39. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-5.1(c)(6) and (g), what constitutes improper sexual contact by a psychotherapist in the second degree, and how is it penalized?

- **A)** Knowingly engaging in verbal sexual flirtation with a client; punished as a simple misdemeanor with up to ninety days in jail
- **B)** Knowingly soliciting nude photographs from a client without in-person physical contact; punished as a standard misdemeanor
- **C)** Knowingly engaging in sexual contact (excluding sexually explicit conduct) with a psychotherapy client; punished as a high and aggravated misdemeanor (felony 1 to 5 years upon repeat conviction)
- **D)** Knowingly engaging in sexually explicit conduct with a client; punished as a capital felony with mandatory minimum twenty-five years

### Q40. Under Rule 510-5-.07, what specific issues must telepsychology informed consent address?

- **A)** An explicit agreement that the client waives all rights to confidential health records and data encryption
- **B)** A complete technical specification of the computer hardware and network router firewalls used by the client
- **C)** Standard boilerplate office policies regarding fee collection and missed appointment cancellation fees only
- **D)** Unique concerns: technologies used, professional boundaries, procedures for responding to electronic communications, and risks

### Q41. Under OCGA 19-7-5, within what period must a parent come forward after a child is found without identification to avoid a finding of abandonment?

- **A)** Six months
- **B)** 30 calendar days
- **C)** One calendar year
- **D)** Three months

### Q42. What happens if an audited licensee fails to document CE?

- **A)** The licensee is placed on indefinite probationary status without fine
- **B)** License will not be renewed; may obtain credits within 31 days
- **C)** The licensee must re-take the EPPP examination within 6 months
- **D)** The license is immediately revoked with no cure period permitted

### Q43. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.09 (Rule 510-4-.02), what is a psychologist's ethical responsibility when cooperation with other professionals is indicated?

- **A)** Condition cooperation on the other professional adopting the psychologist's theoretical orientation
- **B)** Cooperate with other professionals in order to serve their clients/patients effectively and appropriately
- **C)** Demand sole decision-making authority and refuse to share any clinical treatment summaries
- **D)** Require the client to terminate all contact with other medical providers before initiating therapy

### Q44. Under APA Ethics Code Standards 9.04 and 9.11 (Rule 510-4-.02), what is the key ethical distinction between 'test data' and 'test materials'?

- **A)** Test data refers exclusively to computerized interpretive software algorithms, whereas test materials refers to patient interview notes
- **B)** Test data may never be released even with a signed client waiver, whereas test materials must be released to clients upon demand
- **C)** Test data must be destroyed immediately following report generation, whereas test materials must be retained in the clinical file for seven years
- **D)** Test data includes raw/scaled scores and client responses, whereas test materials refers to manuals, instruments, and test questions (which must be secured)

### Q45. Under O.C.G.A. § 24-5-501(a)(6), does the psychologist-patient privilege apply to communications made during a court-ordered psychological evaluation?

- **A)** No; privilege does not attach to court-ordered evaluations, provided the examinee was informed prior to evaluation that communications would not be confidential
- **B)** Yes; provided the examinee signed a formal revocation of consent prior to the final evaluation session
- **C)** No; Georgia courts have entirely abolished psychologist privilege in all civil and criminal proceedings
- **D)** Yes; psychologist-patient privilege in Georgia is absolute and shields court-ordered evaluations from judicial disclosure

### Q46. What must records include?

- **A)** Identifying/contact data, fees/billing, informed consent, diagnosis/basis, plan, dates/services, interventions, assessment
- **B)** Client personal tax returns and detailed credit card account histories
- **C)** Photographs of the client and personal contact details of all extended family members
- **D)** Complete transcripts of all spoken dialogue and personal feelings of the therapist

### Q47. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-3(d)(2), what penalty is mandated upon a second or subsequent conviction for felony statutory rape in Georgia?

- **A)** Mandatory sentence of life imprisonment, or a split sentence with life probation and electronic monitoring
- **B)** Imprisonment for 5 to 10 years with standard administrative parole eligibility
- **C)** Indefinite civil commitment under the Sexually Dangerous Predator Act
- **D)** A non-negotiable fine of $50,000 and 3 years in a county correctional diversion center

### Q48. Under Board Rule 510-5-.06, must a psychological supervisee inform clients of their supervisory status and right to speak with the supervisor?

- **A)** No; disclosure is required only if the client specifically questions the supervisee's credentials
- **B)** Yes; supervisees must inform clients of their supervisory status and right to speak with the supervising psychologist
- **C)** Yes; but only when services are rendered in inpatient psychiatric hospital facilities
- **D)** No; supervisees should present themselves as independent practitioners to prevent undermining therapeutic rapport

### Q49. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.05(b) (Rule 510-4-.02), what disclosure standard applies when revealing confidential information to obtain payment for services?

- **A)** The psychologist must release the client's complete psychotherapeutic session notes and test raw data
- **B)** Disclosure is unrestricted and the collection agency may publicize the clinical diagnosis
- **C)** Disclosure must be limited strictly to the minimum necessary to achieve the collection purpose
- **D)** The psychologist may disclose all collateral family interview notes and developmental history records

### Q50. What is the current legal status of O.C.G.A. § 43-39-11 in the Georgia psychology statutes?

- **A)** Suspended; it is subject to triennial legislative renewal under sunset review provisions
- **B)** Reserved; the former statutory section was repealed in 1986
- **C)** Active; it defines the scope of neuropsychological test administration by psychometrists
- **D)** Active; it sets forth the statutory reciprocity standards for Canadian psychologists
