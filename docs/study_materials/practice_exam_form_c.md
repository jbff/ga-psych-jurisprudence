## Section 1: Examination Questions

### Q1. What exam must be passed before taking the GAMB jurisprudence exam?

- **A)** ABPP Specialty Board Examination
- **B)** The Georgia Board oral examination
- **C)** Comprehensive Psychology Competency Examination (CPCE)
- **D)** EPPP (the national licensing exam)

### Q2. Under Board Rule 510-5-.02, how is a 'professional relationship' defined between a psychologist and a recipient of services?

- **A)** A relationship that exists only after a minimum of ten consecutive therapy sessions have been completed
- **B)** Any commercial contract where money is exchanged regardless of mental health service provision
- **C)** A mutually agreed upon relationship between a psychologist and a patient, client, student, supervisee, or examinee
- **D)** Any casual social conversation occurring between a psychologist and a member of the public

### Q3. What is psychologists' responsibility regarding test security?

- **A)** Make reasonable efforts to maintain integrity and security consistent with law
- **B)** Discard all test protocols immediately after scoring to prevent third-party access
- **C)** Test questions and protocols should be made available online for public transparency
- **D)** Release test manuals and test protocols to attorneys upon receipt of a standard civil subpoena

### Q4. What immunity do Georgia psychology board members have?

- **A)** No civil liability for acts performed in good faith in official duties
- **B)** Qualified immunity from criminal prosecution only, with full civil tort liability
- **C)** Limited immunity that applies only if the Attorney General pre-approves each regulatory decision
- **D)** Absolute immunity covering intentional torts and gross negligence in the course of proceedings

### Q5. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-5.1(d), is client consent an admissible defense to criminal prosecution for improper sexual contact by a psychotherapist?

- **A)** No; unless the psychotherapeutic relationship was formally terminated in writing at least six months prior
- **B)** No; consent of the victim shall not be a defense to criminal prosecution under this statute
- **C)** Yes; documented informed consent signed prior to the sexual contact serves as a complete affirmative defense
- **D)** Yes; client consent is an absolute defense provided both parties are competent adults over age twenty-one

### Q6. May an unlicensed person use titles implying licensure?

- **A)** Yes, provided the title is used exclusively in marketing brochures
- **B)** No; an unlicensed person may not use titles or descriptions tending to convey the impression of psychology licensure
- **C)** Yes, if the word 'consultant' is appended to the title
- **D)** Yes, if the individual holds a master's degree in clinical psychology

### Q7. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-15, under what conditions may the Board waive continuing education requirements for a licensee?

- **A)** Whenever the licensee earns more than $150,000 in gross annual clinical billing receipts
- **B)** The Board has no statutory authority to waive continuing education under any circumstances
- **C)** In cases of hardship, disability, illness, or other appropriate circumstances demonstrated to the Board
- **D)** Whenever the licensee has practiced psychology in Georgia for at least five consecutive years

### Q8. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 2.06(a) (Rule 510-4-.02), what must psychologists refrain from doing when their personal problems may impair work performance?

- **A)** Refrain from seeking independent psychiatric treatment or medical care for the personal problems
- **B)** Refrain from providing psychotherapy to adults while continuing to conduct juvenile evaluations
- **C)** Refrain from initiating activities when there is a substantial likelihood personal problems will prevent competent performance
- **D)** Refrain from billing third-party insurance payors while continuing regular therapy sessions

### Q9. Which of the following is a statutory ground for license revocation under OCGA 43-39-13?

- **A)** Failure to join or maintain membership in the Georgia Psychological Association
- **B)** Refusing to provide pro bono psychological services to indigent clients annually
- **C)** Charging fees exceeding standard Medicare or commercial insurance reimbursement rates
- **D)** Practicing under a false or assumed name

### Q10. May a Georgia psychologist supervise someone who surrendered a license due to discipline?

- **A)** Yes, provided the supervisee practices exclusively in private outpatient clinics
- **B)** Yes, if the supervisee signs an agreement not to bill commercial insurance
- **C)** No, unless Board waives/modifies for extraordinary circumstances
- **D)** Yes, automatically after a six-month waiting period

### Q11. Must a supervisee inform clients of their role and right to speak with the supervisor?

- **A)** No; supervisees should present themselves as independent licensed practitioners
- **B)** Only in inpatient psychiatric hospital facilities
- **C)** Yes; supervisees must inform clients of their supervisory status and right to speak with the supervising psychologist
- **D)** Only if the client specifically questions the supervisee's credentials

### Q12. If licensed in the second year of the biennium, how many CE credits are required for first renewal?

- **A)** 10 credits, with no ethics requirement
- **B)** 20 credits, including 3 ethics credits
- **C)** No continuing education credits are required for the first renewal cycle
- **D)** 6 credits in professional ethics only

### Q13. What is 'test data' under the APA Ethics Code?

- **A)** Raw and scaled scores, client responses, and psychologist notes/recordings during examination
- **B)** Billing codes, clinical appointment logs, and fee payment receipts
- **C)** Published psychometric test manuals, test protocols, stimuli, and test questions
- **D)** Peer-reviewed validation studies evaluating norm samples and reliability coefficients

### Q14. Under Board Rule 510-2-.01, what must an applicant complete before retaking the examination after three unsuccessful EPPP attempts?

- **A)** Earn in person a minimum of 9 semester hours of graduate coursework or 45 hours of approved CE
- **B)** Complete a new 1,500-hour postdoctoral supervised work experience under a new primary supervisor
- **C)** Retake and pass the Georgia Jurisprudence Examination before any subsequent EPPP attempt
- **D)** Earn in person a minimum of 3 semester hours of Board-approved graduate coursework or 15 hours of approved CE

### Q15. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-7(10), which agency has sole regulatory authority over testing and assessment performed by licensed professional counselors and social workers?

- **A)** The Georgia Composite Board of Professional Counselors, Social Workers, and Marriage and Family Therapists
- **B)** The Georgia Composite Medical Board
- **C)** The Professional Standards Commission of the Georgia Department of Education
- **D)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists

### Q16. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 4.05(b) (Rule 510-4-.02), what disclosure standard applies when revealing confidential information to obtain payment for services?

- **A)** Disclosure must be limited strictly to the minimum necessary to achieve the collection purpose
- **B)** Disclosure is unrestricted and the collection agency may publicize the clinical diagnosis
- **C)** The psychologist may disclose all collateral family interview notes and developmental history records
- **D)** The psychologist must release the client's complete psychotherapeutic session notes and test raw data

### Q17. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-1-19, may a licensing board refuse to grant a license to an applicant without a prior administrative hearing?

- **A)** Yes; the board may deny any license arbitrarily without providing any statutory basis or opportunity to be heard
- **B)** No; the Georgia Administrative Procedure Act requires a full jury trial prior to any initial license denial
- **C)** No; an applicant is automatically granted a license if the board fails to hold a formal hearing within 10 days
- **D)** Yes; the board may refuse to grant a license without a prior hearing if not satisfied with qualifications, but the applicant may request an appearance

### Q18. What standard governs disclosures of confidential information without consent?

- **A)** Disclosures are permitted whenever advantageous to the psychologist's academic research
- **B)** Only as mandated by law or permitted by law for valid purposes, limited to the minimum necessary
- **C)** Psychologists may disclose confidential information whenever requested by family members
- **D)** Disclosures without consent are strictly prohibited under every statutory provision

### Q19. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.03 (Rule 510-4-.02), what conduct is prohibited regarding unfair discrimination and harassment?

- **A)** Unfair discrimination and harassment or demeaning conduct based on protected personal characteristics
- **B)** Charging different sliding-scale hourly professional fees based on verified household income levels
- **C)** Referring clients with complex medical comorbidities to board-certified medical specialists
- **D)** Selecting specialized psychological assessment batteries based on the client's primary language

### Q20. May psychologists barter with clients?

- **A)** Yes, but only for tangible goods and never for personal or professional services
- **B)** Yes, provided bartered services are exchanged at 50% fair market cash value
- **C)** Only if not clinically contraindicated and not exploitative
- **D)** Bartering is strictly prohibited under all circumstances in Georgia psychology

### Q21. To whom may test data be released without a client release?

- **A)** Only as required by law or court order
- **B)** Any licensed healthcare professional requesting records by telephone
- **C)** Any family member who pays for the psychological evaluation
- **D)** The client's employer upon submission of a formal workplace inquiry

### Q22. What must records include?

- **A)** Client personal tax returns and detailed credit card account histories
- **B)** Identifying/contact data, fees/billing, informed consent, diagnosis/basis, plan, dates/services, interventions, assessment
- **C)** Photographs of the client and personal contact details of all extended family members
- **D)** Complete transcripts of all spoken dialogue and personal feelings of the therapist

### Q23. What must assessment instruments have for the population tested?

- **A)** Public-domain status allowing unrestricted photocopying of test stimuli
- **B)** Established validity and reliability; if not, describe limitations
- **C)** Formal endorsement and certification by the Georgia Department of Education
- **D)** Universal cultural applicability without need for normative adaptations

### Q24. What must psychologists clarify at the outset of third-party-requested services?

- **A)** The specific billing codes to be submitted to commercial health insurance
- **B)** The complete psychometric test battery to be administered during assessment
- **C)** Role, who is the client, probable uses of information, and limits of confidentiality
- **D)** The identity of all expert witnesses who may testify in subsequent litigation

### Q25. To whom must reports of disabled adult/elder abuse be made?

- **A)** Exclusively to the Georgia composite medical board within 24 hours
- **B)** The Department of Community Health and the Secretary of State
- **C)** Adult protection agency and appropriate law enforcement or prosecuting attorney
- **D)** The Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists

### Q26. Are reports of disabled adult/elder abuse immune from liability?

- **A)** Yes, good-faith reporters are immune from civil/criminal liability
- **B)** Immune from civil damages only, but subject to criminal misdemeanor prosecution
- **C)** Only if the reporter obtained an advance written opinion from legal counsel
- **D)** No; reporters may be sued for defamation if the investigation substantiates no abuse

### Q27. Under Rule 510-5-.07, what specific issues must telepsychology informed consent address?

- **A)** Standard boilerplate office policies regarding fee collection and missed appointment cancellation fees only
- **B)** Unique concerns: technologies used, professional boundaries, procedures for responding to electronic communications, and risks
- **C)** An explicit agreement that the client waives all rights to confidential health records and data encryption
- **D)** A complete technical specification of the computer hardware and network router firewalls used by the client

### Q28. Do the Georgia codes apply to applicants and students?

- **A)** No; the codes apply strictly to fully licensed psychologists holding active licenses
- **B)** No; students and applicants are governed solely by university honor codes
- **C)** Yes; they apply to all licensees and applicants, including conduct during required education/training/employment
- **D)** Only to postdoctoral fellows who have completed doctoral degrees

### Q29. What is required to practice telepsychology in Georgia?

- **A)** Registration with the Federal Communications Commission (FCC)
- **B)** A valid license in any U.S. state without notification or PSYPACT authority
- **C)** Current active Georgia license or PSYPACT authorization
- **D)** A national telebehavioral health certificate with no state license

### Q30. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 6.05 (Rule 510-4-.02), when is bartering for psychological services ethically permissible?

- **A)** Under no circumstances, as bartering is strictly prohibited in Georgia psychological practice
- **B)** Only if it is not clinically contraindicated and the resulting arrangement is not exploitative
- **C)** Only when approved in advance by a formal written consent order issued by the Georgia Board
- **D)** Only when the fair market value of the bartered goods or services does not exceed fifty dollars

### Q31. What happens if an impaired licensee refuses evaluation/rehabilitation?

- **A)** The licensee is granted an automatic 12-month leave of absence
- **B)** The Board must dismiss all pending complaints with prejudice
- **C)** The matter is referred to the APA Ethics Committee for binding arbitration
- **D)** May constitute basis for disciplinary sanctions

### Q32. Under O.C.G.A. § 30-5-4(d), does statutory or professional privilege prevent mandatory reporting of elder or disabled adult abuse?

- **A)** Yes; communications between a psychologist and an adult client are completely confidential and immune from reporting
- **B)** No; abuse, neglect, or exploitation must be reported notwithstanding any privileged communication (except clergy confession)
- **C)** Yes; reporting is prohibited unless the disabled adult or elder person provides signed informed consent to report
- **D)** No; however, reporting is excused if the psychologist determines that reporting would harm therapeutic rapport

### Q33. What is the current legal status of O.C.G.A. § 43-39-11 in the Georgia psychology statutes?

- **A)** Reserved; the former statutory section was repealed in 1986
- **B)** Active; it defines the scope of neuropsychological test administration by psychometrists
- **C)** Suspended; it is subject to triennial legislative renewal under sunset review provisions
- **D)** Active; it sets forth the statutory reciprocity standards for Canadian psychologists

### Q34. What happens if an audited licensee fails to document CE?

- **A)** License will not be renewed; may obtain credits within 31 days
- **B)** The license is immediately revoked with no cure period permitted
- **C)** The licensee is placed on indefinite probationary status without fine
- **D)** The licensee must re-take the EPPP examination within 6 months

### Q35. May psychologists have sexual intimacies with close relatives/significant others of current clients?

- **A)** Yes, if the relative resides in a different household from the client
- **B)** Yes, because relatives are not direct clients of the psychologist
- **C)** No; psychologists may not engage in sexual intimacies with individuals known to be close relatives or significant others of current clients
- **D)** Yes, provided the client gives written permission

### Q36. Under Board Rule 510-3-.02, which national examination parts are required for endorsement applicants who were originally licensed on or after November 1, 2020?

- **A)** The ABPP specialty board examination in lieu of any national written examinations
- **B)** The GRE Subject Test in Psychology and the Miller Analogies Test
- **C)** Both EPPP Part 1 (Knowledge) and Part 2 (Skills)
- **D)** EPPP Part 1 (Knowledge) only, as Part 2 is entirely optional for all endorsement candidates

### Q37. Under Rule 510-5-.08, what must forensic assessments, recommendations, and reports be based on?

- **A)** Subjective impressions and secondhand hearsay reported by retaining litigation attorneys
- **B)** Actuarial recidivism instruments administered without any individual collateral record review
- **C)** Information and techniques sufficient to substantiate findings, including personal interviews when appropriate
- **D)** Polygraph examination results combined with character references submitted by family members

### Q38. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-5.1(f), what is the statutory penalty for improper sexual contact by a psychotherapist in the first degree?

- **A)** Imprisonment for 1 to 25 years and a fine not to exceed $100,000 (10 to 30 years if the victim is under 16)
- **B)** Imprisonment for 1 to 10 years and a fine not to exceed $50,000 (10 to 25 years if the victim is under 16)
- **C)** Imprisonment for 5 to 10 years and a fine not to exceed $25,000 (10 to 20 years if the victim is under 16)
- **D)** Imprisonment for 1 to 5 years and a fine not to exceed $10,000 (5 to 10 years if the victim is under 16)

### Q39. What must psychologists do before consulting about a client?

- **A)** Conduct the consultation exclusively in public professional peer review meetings
- **B)** Obtain a notarized release signed by the client and both of the client's parents
- **C)** Disclose the client's full legal name, social security number, and home address
- **D)** Not disclose identifying information unless consent obtained or unavoidable, and limit to necessary extent

### Q40. Under OCGA 30-5-4, if a mandated reporter of disabled adult or elder abuse is on the staff of a hospital or facility, who makes the report?

- **A)** The staff member notifies the person in charge or designee, who then reports or causes the report to be made
- **B)** The facility's board of directors must convene a formal meeting to vote on making a report
- **C)** The facility's risk management legal counsel evaluates the incident and decides whether to report
- **D)** The staff member must directly contact law enforcement without notifying facility administrators

### Q41. What is the plagiarism rule?

- **A)** Do not present another's work or data as one's own, even if cited
- **B)** Plagiarism rules apply only to published books and not journal articles or dissertations
- **C)** Presenting another's work is permitted if paraphrased without citation
- **D)** Borrowing up to 25% of another's text without attribution is considered fair use

### Q42. Under APA Ethics Code Standard 3.09 (Rule 510-4-.02), what is a psychologist's ethical responsibility when cooperation with other professionals is indicated?

- **A)** Demand sole decision-making authority and refuse to share any clinical treatment summaries
- **B)** Cooperate with other professionals in order to serve their clients/patients effectively and appropriately
- **C)** Condition cooperation on the other professional adopting the psychologist's theoretical orientation
- **D)** Require the client to terminate all contact with other medical providers before initiating therapy

### Q43. How many participants must comprise an ongoing group consultation group under Area II?

- **A)** Minimum 3 psychologists and up to a maximum of 8 licensed participants
- **B)** Minimum 4 psychologists and up to a maximum of 12 licensed participants
- **C)** Minimum 5 psychologists with no upper limit on licensed participants
- **D)** Minimum 2 psychologists and up to a maximum of 5 licensed participants

### Q44. Under O.C.G.A. § 43-39-7(9), does a psychologist practicing under the authority of PSYPACT need to obtain a standard Georgia license?

- **A)** Yes; PSYPACT practitioners must complete the Georgia Jurisprudence Examination and obtain a full Georgia license
- **B)** No; practicing pursuant to the Psychology Interjurisdictional Compact (PSYPACT) is an express statutory exception to Georgia licensure
- **C)** No; but PSYPACT practitioners must maintain a full-time physical clinical office located within the State of Georgia
- **D)** Yes; PSYPACT practitioners must submit an endorsement application to the Board within thirty days of initiating practice

### Q45. Under O.C.G.A. § 16-6-5.1(c)(6) and (g), what constitutes improper sexual contact by a psychotherapist in the second degree, and how is it penalized?

- **A)** Knowingly engaging in verbal sexual flirtation with a client; punished as a simple misdemeanor with up to ninety days in jail
- **B)** Knowingly engaging in sexual contact (excluding sexually explicit conduct) with a psychotherapy client; punished as a high and aggravated misdemeanor (felony 1 to 5 years upon repeat conviction)
- **C)** Knowingly engaging in sexually explicit conduct with a client; punished as a capital felony with mandatory minimum twenty-five years
- **D)** Knowingly soliciting nude photographs from a client without in-person physical contact; punished as a standard misdemeanor

### Q46. Under Board Rule 510-8-.02, how many continuing education credits may be earned for first or second authoring of a peer-reviewed journal article?

- **A)** A maximum of 3 CE credits per article, up to a total of 6 CE credits per biennium
- **B)** A maximum of 15 CE credits per article, up to a total of 30 CE credits per biennium
- **C)** A maximum of 5 CE credits per article, up to a total of 10 CE credits per biennium
- **D)** A maximum of 10 CE credits per article, up to a total of 20 CE credits per biennium

### Q47. What must psychologists base assessment opinions on?

- **A)** Testimonials and collateral reports from interested third-party family members
- **B)** Statistical averages of the demographic group without individualized testing
- **C)** Intuitive clinical impressions gathered during an informal initial meeting
- **D)** Information and techniques sufficient to substantiate findings

### Q48. Under OCGA 19-7-5, within what period must a parent come forward after a child is found without identification to avoid a finding of abandonment?

- **A)** Three months
- **B)** 30 calendar days
- **C)** Six months
- **D)** One calendar year

### Q49. Under Rule 510-2-.04, how many in-person coursework hours are required to demonstrate residency equivalency?

- **A)** Minimum 1,000 in-person coursework hours
- **B)** Minimum 750 in-person coursework hours
- **C)** Minimum 250 in-person coursework hours
- **D)** Minimum 540 in-person coursework hours

### Q50. Are impairment exam results privileged from board proceedings?

- **A)** No; admissible despite any privilege claim
- **B)** Yes; privileged unless a superior court judge issues an ex parte unsealing order
- **C)** Yes; protected by the statutory psychologist-patient privilege under OCGA 43-39-16
- **D)** Yes; admissible only if the licensee explicitly waives physician-patient confidentiality in writing
