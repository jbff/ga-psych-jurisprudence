1. Open Anki 2.1 and choose **File > Import**.
2. Select `gamb_deck.tsv` and set **Note type** to **Basic**.
3. Ensure fields map to `front`, `back`, and `tags`; choose an existing deck named **GA Jurisprudence GAMB** (or create it).
4. Deck contains **472 cards** drawn exclusively from the official Georgia jurisprudence corpus (all 44 OCGA statutes + all 10 Department 510 Rule chapters).
5. **Zero non-testable fluff**: Testing center logistics, fees, dress codes, and application forms have been purged. Every card tests substantive legal facts.
6. Tagged by the official 6 exam outline domains:
   - `licensure` (218 cards)
   - `competence` (104 cards)
   - `prof-relationships` (73 cards)
   - `assessment` (30 cards)
   - `privacy` (24 cards)
   - `recordkeeping` (23 cards)
7. Study schedule: add ~30 new cards/day starting today to finish new-card introductions in ~14 days and be review-clean by November 6.
8. Each card is atomic: one fact per card, with an exact legal citation such as `(OCGA 43-39-7)` or `(Rule 510-5-.04)`.
