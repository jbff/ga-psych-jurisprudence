# GEORGIA PSYCHOLOGY JURISPRUDENCE EXAMINATION — SYLLABUS & TOPIC MAPPING

This document outlines the substantive subject matter covered on the Georgia Psychology Jurisprudence Examination. It contains **ALL and ONLY** the testable domains, topic areas, and primary legal citations—omitting administrative logistics (test fees, center directions, registration procedures) that are not tested on the examination.

---

## 1. Exam Structure & Content Distribution

The examination consists of **50 scored multiple-choice questions** distributed across six content domains:

| Domain | Domain Title | Item Count | Percentage |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---:|
| **I** | Professional Relationships | 14 | 28% |
| **II** | Licensure and Regulations | 13 | 26% |
| **III** | Competence | 9 | 18% |
| **IV** | Assessment | 6 | 12% |
| **V** | Privacy and Confidentiality | 5 | 10% |
| **VI** | Recordkeeping and Fees | 3 | 6% |
| **Total** | | **50** | **100%** |

*Passing Score: 38 out of 50 (76%).*

## 2. Official Reference List (Primary Legal Authorities)

The Board's official examination specifications derive questions exclusively from two primary legal sources:

### A. Official Code of Georgia Annotated (OCGA)
1. **Psychology Practice Act:**
   - `OCGA § 43-39-1` through `§ 43-39-20` (Definitions, Board creation, powers, licensure requirements, examination, reciprocity, renewals, disciplinary grounds/hearings, temporary/provisional licenses, CE, privileged communications, title restrictions, injunctions, penalties, immunity).
2. **General Licensing Provisions:**
   - `OCGA § 43-1-19` through `§ 43-1-25`, and `§ 43-1-27` (Disciplinary authority of professional licensing boards, hearings, subpoenas, reprimands, probation, emergency suspension, corporate practice rules, and § 43-1-27 mandatory 10-day notification of felony convictions). *Note: § 43-1-26 is reserved in the current Georgia code.*
3. **Sexual Misconduct / Crimes:**
   - `OCGA § 16-6-5.1` (Improper sexual contact by employee, agent, psychotherapist, or person in position of trust; consent is not a defense; 1st & 2nd degree felony penalties).
4. **Controlled Substances Sanctions:**
   - `OCGA § 16-13-110` through `§ 16-13-114` (Sanctions against licensed individuals for offenses involving controlled substances or marijuana; mandatory suspension/revocation rules).
5. **Mandated Child Abuse Reporting:**
   - `OCGA § 19-7-5` (Mandated reporting of suspected child abuse; reporting immediately but no later than 24 hours; immunity; override of privilege).
6. **Privileged Communications:**
   - `OCGA § 24-9-21` (Statutory privilege placing psychologist-client relations on the same basis as attorney-client; codified at § 24-5-501 in post-2013 code).
7. **Protection of Disabled Adults & Elder Persons:**
   - `OCGA § 30-5-4` (Mandatory reporting of abuse, neglect, or exploitation of disabled adults or elders; immediate reporting; statutory immunity; privilege override).
8. **Health Service Provider Psychologists & Hospital Practice:**
   - `OCGA § 31-7-160` through `§ 31-7-165` (Definition, hospital staff privileges, qualifications, training requirements, orders for care/treatment).
9. **Emergency Mental Health Admissions (Form 1013):**
   - `OCGA § 37-3-41` (Involuntary emergency evaluation/admission; psychologist authority equal to physician; 48-hour examination requirement; 7-day certificate validity; 72-hour peace officer execution).

### B. Rules of the Georgia State Board of Examiners of Psychologists (Department 510)
- `Chapter 510-1`: Organization
- `Chapter 510-2`: Licensure by Examination
- `Chapter 510-3`: Licensure by Endorsement
- `Chapter 510-4`: Code of Ethics (Adopts the APA *Ethical Principles of Psychologists and Code of Conduct*)
- `Chapter 510-5`: Supplemental Code of Conduct
- `Chapter 510-6`: Disciplinary Hearings
- `Chapter 510-7`: Renewal/Reinstatement/Inactive License
- `Chapter 510-8`: Continuing Education Requirements
- `Chapter 510-9`: Licenses of Limited Duration
- `Chapter 510-10`: Unlicensed Practice

## 3. Detailed Topic Breakdown by Domain & Primary Citations

### Domain I: Professional Relationships (14 Items)
- **Avoiding Harm:** APA Ethics Code Standard 3.04; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.01.
- **Multiple Relationships:** APA Standard 3.05; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.01 (concurrent and consecutive dual roles, business relationships with clients, objectivity impairment).
- **Conflicts of Interest:** APA Standard 3.06; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.01.
- **Therapy Provided to Those Served by Others:** APA Standard 10.04; Rule 510-4-.02 (consultation and client welfare when client is receiving services elsewhere).
- **Informed Consent to Therapy:** APA Standard 10.01; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.06 (nature, anticipated course, fees, third-party involvement, confidentiality limits).
- **Supervisory Relationships:** Rule 510-5-.06; Rule 510-2-.05; APA Standard 7.07; APA Standard 3.08 (supervisor responsibility, 3-supervisee maximum without Board approval, written supervisory agreements, client notification of supervisee status).
- **Sexual Misconduct (Laws):** OCGA § 16-6-5.1 (psychotherapist sexual contact is a felony; consent not a defense).
- **Sexual Misconduct (Relationships):** APA Standards 10.05, 10.06, 10.07, 10.08; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.01(2) (absolute prohibition with current clients; 2-year post-termination minimum ban with severe restrictions thereafter; prohibitions with relatives/significant others and therapy with former sexual partners).
- **Student Disclosure of Personal Information:** APA Standard 7.04; Rule 510-4-.02.
- **Informed Consent to Research:** APA Standards 8.02, 8.03, 8.04; Rule 510-4-.02.
- **Research & Publication Relationships:** APA Standards 8.05, 8.11, 8.12; Rule 510-4-.02 (authorship, publication credits, plagiarism).
- **Definitions of Relationships:** Rule 510-5-.02; Rule 510-5-.06 (client, patient, supervisee, organization).
- **Referrals & Client Consideration:** APA Standard 3.09; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.06.
- **Services Delivered to or Through Organizations:** APA Standard 3.11; Rule 510-4-.02 (clarifying roles, confidentiality limits, intended recipients).
- **Third-Party Requests for Services:** APA Standard 3.07; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.08(5) (clarifying client identity, role, probable uses, limits of confidentiality).
- **Continuity of Care (Interruption/Termination):** APA Standards 3.12, 10.09, 10.10; Rule 510-5-.06(2) (professional will/coverage plan for illness, death, unavailability, termination pre-termination counseling).

### Domain II: Licensure and Regulations (13 Items)
- **License Requirements:** OCGA § 43-39-8; Rule 510-2-.01 through .05 (doctoral degree, 2,000 hours internship, 2,000 hours postdoctoral/predoctoral supervised experience, EPPP 1 & 2, Jurisprudence, Oral).
- **Unlicensed Practice & Title Restrictions:** OCGA § 43-39-7; OCGA § 43-39-17; Rule 510-10-.01 through .04 (misdemeanor violation; restrictions on title "psychologist").
- **Exemptions from Licensure:** OCGA § 43-39-7 (school psychologists in educational institutions, academic teaching/research without direct clinical service, religious organizations, trainees under supervision, other licensed professions).
- **Limited Practice (Out-of-State Psychologists):** OCGA § 43-39-7(8); Rule 510-9-.03 (maximum 30 days per calendar year; 5 days advance notice to Board).
- **PSYPACT Practice:** OCGA § 43-39-7(9); OCGA §§ 43-39-21, 43-39-22 (telepsychology and temporary in-person practice under PSYPACT).
- **Temporary & Provisional Licenses:** OCGA § 43-39-14; Rules 510-9-.01, 510-9-.02 (temporary license valid for 12 months, non-renewable; provisional license valid for up to 24 months for postdoc experience).
- **Renewal, Inactive Status, & Reinstatement:** OCGA § 43-39-12; Rule 510-7-.01, .02, .03 (biennial renewal by Dec 31 of even years; 40 CE hours; late renewal through April 30; reinstatement requirements).
- **Supervisory Requirements:** Rule 510-2-.05; Rule 510-5-.06 (primary supervisor limits, 1 hour weekly individual supervision, direct responsibility).
- **Reporting Child Abuse:** OCGA § 19-7-5 (mandatory reporter status; immediate reporting, not later than 24 hours; reports to DFCS/law enforcement; immunity; privilege override).
- **Reporting Elder & Disabled Adult Abuse:** OCGA § 30-5-4 (mandatory reporting; immediate notification; reports to APS and law enforcement; statutory immunity; privilege override).
- **Delegation of Work to Others:** APA Standard 2.05; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.06(3) (delegating only to competent individuals; avoiding dual roles; maintaining responsibility).
- **Advertising & Public Statements:** APA Standards 5.01, 5.02, 5.06; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.09(2) (prohibition of false, fraudulent, or misleading statements, claims of unique abilities, or unearned credentials).
- **Avoiding Deceptive Statements About Credentials:** APA Standard 5.01(b); Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.09(2).
- **Powers of the Licensing Board:** OCGA § 43-39-6; OCGA § 43-39-13; OCGA § 43-1-19 (subpoenas, oaths, establishing rules, investigations, emergency summary suspensions).
- **Disciplinary Actions & Sanctions:** OCGA § 43-39-13; OCGA § 43-1-19; Rule 510-6-.01 (reprimand, probation, fines up to $500 per violation, suspension, revocation).
- **Felony & Controlled Substance Convictions:** OCGA § 43-1-27 (mandatory notification of felony conviction within 10 days); OCGA § 16-13-111 (controlled substance sanctions: minimum 3-month suspension for 1st offense, revocation for 2nd offense).
- **Immunity:** OCGA § 43-39-2(b) (Board member immunity); OCGA § 43-39-20 (immunity for good-faith testimony); OCGA § 19-7-5(f) (child abuse reporting immunity); OCGA § 30-5-4(c) (elder/disabled abuse reporting immunity).

### Domain III: Competence (9 Items)
- **Practice Within Boundaries of Competence:** APA Standard 2.01; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.03(1) (education, training, supervised experience, consultation).
- **Maintaining Competence & Continuing Education:** APA Standard 2.03; Rule 510-5-.03(2); Rule 510-8-.01 (40 hours per biennium; minimum 6 hours in professional ethics).
- **Providing Services in Emergencies:** APA Standard 2.02; Rule 510-4-.02 (services outside training permitted temporarily during emergencies until appropriate care is obtained).
- **Recognizing Personal Problems & Impairment:** APA Standard 2.06; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.05(1) (refraining from practice when impaired; taking corrective action).
- **Resolving Ethical Issues:** APA Standards 1.01, 1.02, 1.03, 1.04, 1.05; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.09(1) (handling conflicts between ethics and law/organizational demands; informal resolution; formal reporting).
- **Electronic Transmission & Telepsychology:** Rule 510-5-.07; APA Standard 2.01; OCGA § 43-39-7(9) (telepractice standards equal to in-person services; security, verification of client identity/location, emergency contacts).
- **Design of Training & Educational Programs:** APA Standards 7.01, 7.02, 7.03; Rule 510-4-.02 (accurate program descriptions; ensuring programs meet claimed licensing goals).

### Domain IV: Assessment (6 Items)
- **Bases for Assessments:** APA Standard 9.01; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.08(2) (opinions based on adequate direct evaluation; documenting limitations when direct examination is not feasible).
- **Use of Assessments:** APA Standard 9.02; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.08 (validity, reliability, appropriate norms, cultural/linguistic considerations).
- **Informed Consent in Assessment:** APA Standard 9.03; Rule 510-4-.02 (nature, purpose, fees, third-party involvement, limits of confidentiality).
- **User Qualifications:** Rule 510-5-.08(1); OCGA § 43-39-1(6) (qualifications required to administer and interpret psychological tests; restrictions against unqualified use).
- **Use of Obsolete Tests & Outdated Test Results:** APA Standard 9.08; Rule 510-4-.02 (prohibition against basing decisions on outdated/obsolete measures).
- **Scoring, Interpreting, and Explaining Assessment Results:** APA Standards 9.06, 9.10; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.08(3) (interpreting limitations, explaining results to examinee or representative).
- **Test Security & Test Data:** APA Standards 9.04, 9.11; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.08(4) (distinction between test data [raw scores, client responses] and test materials [manuals, instruments, protocols, test items]; maintaining test security).
- **Forensic Assessments:** APA Standard 9.01(b); Rule 510-5-.08(5); Rule 510-4-.02 Standard 3.05(c) (clarifying forensic vs therapeutic roles; avoiding conflicting dual roles in court evaluations).

### Domain V: Privacy and Confidentiality (5 Items)
- **Privileged Communications:** OCGA § 43-39-16; OCGA § 24-9-21 (attorney-client privilege standard; nearly absolute privilege under Georgia law).
- **Limits of Confidentiality:** APA Standard 4.02; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.04(1) (discussing limits at outset; mandatory exceptions including child abuse OCGA 19-7-5, elder abuse OCGA 30-5-4, emergency commitment OCGA 37-3-41, court orders).
- **Maintaining Confidentiality & Security:** APA Standard 4.01; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.04 (protecting confidential records in all media).
- **Recordings:** APA Standard 4.03; Rule 510-4-.02 (prior permission required from client or legal representative before audio/video recording).
- **Use of Confidential Information for Didactic / Research Purposes:** APA Standard 4.07; Rule 510-4-.02 (disguising identifying details or obtaining written authorization).

### Domain VI: Recordkeeping and Fees (3 Items)
- **Documentation and Maintenance of Records:** APA Standard 6.01; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.04(2) (creating and maintaining accurate professional records).
- **Record Retention Periods:** Rule 510-5-.04(2) (in the absence of superseding law, records must be retained for a minimum of **7 years** after last date of service for adults; for minors, 7 years past age of majority).
- **Fees and Financial Arrangements:** APA Standard 6.04; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.06(1) (reaching agreement on fees as early as feasible; no misleading billing; collection procedures).
- **Bartering with Clients:** APA Standard 6.05; Rule 510-4-.02; Rule 510-5-.01(1) (bartering permissible only if not clinically contraindicated and not exploitative).
- **Withholding Records for Nonpayment:** APA Standard 6.03; Rule 510-4-.02 (psychologists may NOT withhold records needed for a client's emergency treatment solely because payment is overdue).
- **Accuracy in Reports to Payors:** APA Standard 6.06; Rule 510-4-.02 (accurate description of service nature, fees, diagnosis, provider identity).
